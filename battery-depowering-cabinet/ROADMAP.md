# Battery Depowering Cabinet — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1** · Research plan, not permission to operate. Each phase is conditional on the preceding evidence gate. The [research specification](SPECIFICATION.md) defines the proposed acceptance envelope; the qualified collection-only pathway is the control.

## 1. Concept

Define the labelled, intact, externally accessible rechargeable Li-ion pack envelope (3.6–14.8 V nominal, ≤50 Wh), site and recycler constraints, and a documented hazard analysis covering mis-sorting, runaway, off-gas and emergency response. Exit only when the receiving recycler and independent fire/electrical reviewers agree on testable requirements and a collection-only comparison protocol; otherwise stop.

## 2. Prototype

Build a supervised one-bay instrumented surrogate-load rig with keyed carrier, fuse, current-limited resistor, normally open contactor, redundant thermal channels and measured electrical/airflow trips. Bench-test polarity reversal, sensor opens/shorts, blocked airflow, welded contacts, mains loss and simulated heating before any real pack; exit only after documented fault-injection passes and an independently reviewed, site-specific battery trial protocol.

## 3. Pilot

At an authorized, staffed, fire-engineered site, trial only approved intact packs with trained operators and a written incident plan. Log eligible/rejected counts, operator minutes, endpoint rebound, faults, heat, recycler acceptance and near misses alongside a collection-only control. Exit only if independent review finds acceptable residual risk and a defensible operational advantage; an uncontrolled heat event or false acceptance halts the study.

## 4. Scale

If the pilot succeeds, validate reproducible carrier/profile acceptance, service intervals, heat rejection, procurement cost and installation at multiple qualified sites. Require repeatable production and maintenance tests, site approvals, recycler contracts and independent safety review before expanding; a prototype pass does not establish certification.

## 5. Ubiquity

Conditional long-term ambition: serviceable, audited equipment only at staffed facilities where comparative evidence and local approvals justify onsite discharge. Track long-run incident rates and total lifecycle cost against collection-only logistics, retire ineffective installations and keep unknown/damaged packs on separate specialist routes. Ubiquity is not assumed; a safer collection-only standard is an acceptable outcome.
