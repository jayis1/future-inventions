# Binder-Mapped Sand Reclaimer — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** Each phase is conditional on the prior evidence gate; dates are planning horizons, not promised releases.

## Concept

Document eligible silica sands and organic binder families, rejected contaminants, foundry-specific acceptance tests and full material/energy boundaries. By the end of the 2027–2030 characterization window, independent lab trials must establish at least one repeatable attrition/thermal recipe and a binder-specific off-gas hazard inventory before hardware is ordered.

## Prototype

Build an enclosed, instrumented bench system with a screened feed, attrition stage, small electric bed, separated clean-side heat exchange, exhaust treatment and safe shutdown interlocks. Demonstrate repeatable lot traceability, calibrated scales and meters, representative sampling, and independently tested recovered-sand casting behavior; reject the design if residuals or air emissions cannot be contained.

## Pilot

Run a permitted, independently monitored 500-scheduled-hour trial (target 2031–2035) against a maintained conventional reclaimer on matched eligible feed. Set numerical LOI, sieve, casting-reject, occupational exposure and stack limits with the host foundry and regulator *before* operation; sample every release lot and record all stoppages. Gates: ≥90% accepted output by inlet mass, ≤180 kWh/t purchased electricity including abatement, and ≤10% planned cleaning downtime; stop feed and heat on loss of draft/oxidizer, failed filters, emissions excursions or out-of-spec product and quarantine the affected lot.

## Scale

Only after an audited pilot, replicate at several binder-matched sites (aspirational 2036–2040), with local permits and matched-feed cost and lifecycle comparisons. Maintain a site-level mass balance for accepted sand, rejects and captured dust and a full energy/emissions ledger; expand only where casting quality and net environmental and economic results remain positive under actual transport and grid conditions.

## Ubiquity

For a possible 2050 network, standardize independently audited batch passports and acceptance interfaces while preserving site-specific binder recipes, worker protections and waste permits. Wider adoption is conditional on reliable segregated feed, verified virgin-sand substitution and safe residual treatment; a failed site exits the network rather than diluting release criteria.
