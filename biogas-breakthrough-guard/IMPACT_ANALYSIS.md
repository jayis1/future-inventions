# Biogas Breakthrough Guard — Conditional Impact Analysis

**Author: jayis1** · Scenario arithmetic, not measured performance or a forecast. No prototype or site data exist.

## Boundary and inputs

The boundary is gas passing through a proposed guard, not digester methane capture or household health. A unit operates at **1.0 m³/h** only during the assumed hours; assumed inlet H₂S is within the README's 200–1,000 ppmv test envelope. EPA describes iron-oxide adsorption as an existing low-flow biogas treatment and requires condensate and pressure management [1]. The comparator is a conventional single-bed system with equal qualified media under the same gas conditions; actual comparative breakthrough and cost data remain unknown. Outlet <10 ppmv and pressure drop ≤1 kPa are **unverified design targets**, not scenario results.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Limited | 10 | 1,000 | 200 ppmv | 10,000 | 2.79 | 2,700 |
| Illustrative | 100 | 2,000 | 500 ppmv | 200,000 | 139.4 | 27,000 |
| Extensive | 1,000 | 3,000 | 1,000 ppmv | 3,000,000 | 4,182 | 270,000 |

*Gas volume = units × 1.0 m³/h × hours/unit. For an ideal gas at 25 °C and ~1 atm, molar volume ≈24.45 L/mol; H₂S mass = m³ × 1,000 L/m³ ÷ 24.45 L/mol × inlet ppmv × 10⁻⁶ × 34.08 g/mol ÷ 1,000 g/kg. Rounding is to displayed precision. Inlet mass is a **potential treatment load**, not proven capture, avoided exposure, or avoided emissions. At other temperatures/pressures, recalculate the molar volume and measure actual inlet composition.

**The US$270/unit illustration** = US$220 target hardware + two × US$25 target refill. It assumes two refills per unit per year solely to expose cost sensitivity; true media life and service frequency are unknown. Every additional refill adds US$25/unit/year, or US$250 / US$2,500 / US$25,000 across the three scenarios. Hardware is a one-time factory target, not a recurring annual cost; annualized economics need a measured lifetime. Full-site pressure protection, qualified sensors, calibration, installation, training, labor, energy, freight, downtime, spent-media transport/disposal and certification are excluded and could dominate this subtotal. The claimed factory costs are unquoted 2038 goals at 10,000 units/year, so extrapolating them to ten units is especially speculative.

## What would establish an actual benefit

A matched single-bed control must reveal breakthrough hours and outlet H₂S mass over repeated wet/variable-load cycles. Only the **measured difference** in H₂S delivered beyond the site-specific appliance limit, adjusted for false trips and downtime, is a defensible quality benefit. Measure methane leakage and energy use independently; H₂S removal by itself yields **zero established methane or climate credit**. No avoided illness, prevented death, or additional clean fuel is counted. Compare lifecycle impacts of media manufacture, energy, replacement travel and waste handling before claiming net environmental benefit. A supervised pilot should publish confidence intervals, unsafe-event counts and system-level cost per qualified m³; until then, every adoption and utilization number above is an assumption.
