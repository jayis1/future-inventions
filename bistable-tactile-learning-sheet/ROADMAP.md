# Roadmap — Bistable Tactile Learning Sheet

**Author:** jayis1

**Status:** Proposed development gates; dates and performance figures are targets, not achieved results.

## Phase 1 — Concept

Select the variable-stiffness TPU collar, platinum-cured silicone diaphragm, printed-carbon heater, and eight-sector pneumatic architecture through 20 × 20 coupon arrays. Freeze the safety envelope and verification methods only after demonstrating 0.7–1.0 mm displacement, at least 0.2 N force, a user surface at or below 40°C, and repeatable relatching; the exit gate is <1% command error over 100,000 accelerated cycles with no unsafe failure.

## Phase 2 — Prototype

Build 40 × 30 quarter sheets and then an 80 × 60 engineering unit with the offline renderer, active-matrix driver, reversible pump, feedback sensors, battery, and replaceable cassette. Exit requires typical refresh below 2 seconds, full inversion below 8 seconds, <0.1% error after one retry, two million cycles per pixel with <10% force loss, and successful drop, puncture, humidity, cleaning, thermal-fault, and repair tests.

## Phase 3 — Pilot

Co-design ruggedized units with blind and deafblind learners, teachers, accessibility specialists, and repair technicians, then operate at least 500 devices for two academic years across varied climates and Braille codes. The phase closes only when preregistered studies meet non-inferiority gates against validated embossed controls, critical modules can be replaced in under 15 minutes, privacy and accessibility reviews pass, and uptime, cleaning, failure, and total-cost data are published.

## Phase 4 — Scale

Qualify roll-to-roll backplane production, molded fluidics, regional final assembly, cassette take-back, and a 15-year spare-parts commitment. Scale begins only after automated inspection supports stable yield, audited suppliers and safety certifications are in place, delivered price is US$250–400 at more than 100,000 units per year, and five-year lifecycle cost is competitive with each deployment's actual mix of embossing and line displays.

## Phase 5 — Ubiquity

Establish open tactile-scene interchange, multilingual content libraries, teacher authoring tools, independent conformance labs, local repair training, and equitable public procurement. Ubiquity means interoperable and repairable access rather than a shipment target: regional service coverage, transparent reliability and lifecycle reporting, affordable replacement cassettes, and demonstrated recovery of worn modules must persist without subscriptions, surveillance, or vendor lock-in.
