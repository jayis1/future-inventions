# Brick-Kiln Flue Sulfur Cassette — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1 · Research concept, not installation guidance.** Advancement requires independent engineering and local permits; each phase may end in a no-go.

## Concept

Map candidate kiln flue temperature, actual flow, SO₂ loading, dust, draft and existing control performance across a full firing cycle. Screen clean-fuel and maintained-control alternatives first; reject sites without fan headroom or lawful residue disposal. Set a site-specific test protocol and mass-balance boundary.

## Prototype

Build a contained, temperature-qualified contact and dewatering module; bench-test spray distribution, gas bypass, demister fouling, dirty pressure drop, slurry chemistry and metal leaching. Gate: demonstrate controllable draft and sulfur balance with independently calibrated instrumentation before connecting to a firing kiln.

## Pilot

At five permitted kilns and five comparable existing-control sites, independently measure treated and whole-season SO₂ mass, bypass, PM, CO, fuel/brick, pump/fan energy, water, waste and maintenance. Gate: achieve ≥60% treated-stream removal without worsened worker CO, emissions or fuel intensity; publish all outages and uncertainty, and verify disposal routes.

## Scale

If pilot benefits exceed installed and recurring costs, standardize a small set of site-qualified modules and train local licensed service teams. Independently audit fabrication, fan compatibility, wastewater treatment and service safety; do not claim the US$8,000 workshop target as an installed price.

## Ubiquity

Offer replaceable service modules only to eligible legacy kilns where cleaner fuels or replacement are not better options. Require annual emissions audits, permitted waste take-back, transparent bypass reporting and withdrawal of modules at sites that fail the whole-season benefit or safe-draft test.
