# Brownout-Safe Cooking Rail — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** Research concept, not a certified appliance. Each phase is gated by evidence and qualified electrical review.

## Concept

Specify a 48 V supply envelope, compatible pots, battery-specific undervoltage limits, series fail-open disconnect, thermal cutouts and manual recovery. Interview cooks and electricians in one intended mini-grid setting; exit only with a documented fault tree, recipe/energy budget, comparative test plan and installed-cost baseline.

## Prototype

Build a guarded three-port bench rig with instrumented 250 W loads before using food-contact pots. Inject sag oscillation, welded contacts, sensor faults, shorts, moisture and controller power loss; verify that faults isolate heating, neither automatic restart nor connector overheating occurs, and a 1,000-cycle connector test is completed. Revise the design if any protective function depends solely on software.

## Pilot

After independent electrical and food-contact assessment for the intended jurisdiction, conduct a supervised 10-kitchen randomized crossover against protected conventional electric cooking on the same supply. Measure full meal completion, actual temperatures, fallback fuel, PM₂.₅/CO, energy per completed meal, nuisance trips, connector temperature and user bypass attempts; do not expand while hazardous failures remain unresolved.

## Scale

If the pilot establishes a reproducible improvement without new hazards, qualify replaceable ports and pot liners, document installer training and local repairs, then seek applicable product/installation approvals. Requote the complete installed system against LPG and standard electric pressure cookers; scale in stages only where supply capacity, service availability and actual fuel displacement are verified.

## Ubiquity

Only after multi-season field evidence, independently audited electrical safety and affordable maintenance, publish an interoperable interface and regional repair specifications. By 2050, potential widespread use remains conditional on sufficient clean supply and measured displacement of polluting cooking; retire or redesign deployments that fail these gates.
