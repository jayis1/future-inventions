# Capillary Salt-Export Tile — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** Proposed evidence gates, not completed milestones. See [README.md](README.md).

## Concept

Specify a 10 m² fallow strip, 0.1 m² cassette, 10–20 cm soil contact and a permitted salt-disposal path. Establish a paired soil-column protocol with initial and final 0–20 cm ion inventories, water balance and sham controls; reject sites with inaccessible moisture, shallow saline groundwater, flooding or unsuitable waste routes. Gate: a pre-registered salt/water balance and analytical detection limit capable of distinguishing a 0.5 g/day collection signal from background variation.

## Prototype

Build replaceable ceramic wick/spreader and lidded HDPE/PP collector candidates. Over 90 days, compare equal-starting-salt columns, quantify wick retention, leakage, dry captured ions and moisture depletion, and cycle lids and ceramics through rain, UV and wet–dry tests. Gate: at least 0.5 g/day collected on working days, a demonstrable treated-versus-sham soil-inventory decline with ion-balance closure within laboratory uncertainty, and no uncontained discharge; otherwise redesign or stop.

## Pilot

With landowner and waste-handler consent, randomize 30 fallow 10 m² strips against 30 nearby sham strips for two dry seasons. Measure depth-resolved soluble salts, porewater, groundwater, rainfall, neighboring crop response, service time and actual disposal receipts. Gate: pre-registered positive net 0–20 cm removal, no material water-stress or off-site leakage signal, reproducible service intervals and a full per-gram removal cost competitive with locally quoted alternatives.

## Scale

Only after an independent replicate pilot, qualify local ceramic, HDPE and liner suppliers for repeated salt, rain, abrasion and replacement cycles. Audit installers, batch material compatibility, waste capacity and transport; include assays, labor and replacement in procurement bids. Gate: multiple sites reproduce the pilot's net-removal and containment findings with funded take-back and cost accounting.

## Ubiquity

If replicated performance and legal disposal endure, offer a standard cassette and open test protocol for specifically surveyed fallow margins rather than blanket farmland deployment. Keep independent monitoring, owner consent, worker protection, ceramic/liner take-back and clear retirement criteria funded. This is conditional adoption, not a 2050 forecast or a substitute for drainage and irrigation management.
