# Roadmap — Circular PCB Electrorefinery

## Phase 1 — Concept (2027–2029)

Build separate bench rigs for induction-assisted component release, submerged electrohydraulic delamination, glycine/citrate/thiosulfate leaching, and potential-controlled electrowinning. Use three standardized FR-4 board families to map energy, liberation, selectivity, ligand decay, and brominated byproducts. Advance only after independent analysis confirms **>90% copper-interface liberation**, bounded emissions, and a credible closed mass balance; target program cost: **$3–6 million**.

## Phase 2 — Prototype (2029–2032)

Integrate a **5 kg/day** skid with physically separated electrolytes, automated assay, safety PLC, and batch passports. Process at least 20 board classes and iterate electrode cassettes, seals, filters, and component-handling recipes. Gates are **>95% Cu recovery**, **>90% Au/Ag recovery**, **>98% batch mass closure**, no uncontrolled liquid discharge, and 1,000 operating hours without a recordable chemical release; target cumulative cost: **$12–20 million**.

## Phase 3 — Pilot (2032–2036)

Place five supervised **20 kg/day** systems at repair, municipal, and producer-return facilities in different climates. Independent laboratories measure worker exposure, effluent, products, and full lifecycle impacts for 12 months; regional hubs service all sealed chemical and residue cassettes. Certification requires target recovery across 100 consecutive batches, **>90% availability**, positive unit economics on measured mixed-grade feed, and safe downstream disposition of every output.

## Phase 4 — Scale (2036–2043)

Industrialize 20 and **100 kg/day** modules with interchangeable wet-process skids, standardized training, remote diagnostics that exclude device data, and audited producer-responsibility contracts. Establish regional electrolyte regeneration and precious-metal finishing hubs before selling local units. At cumulative production above 10,000 systems, target **$140,000–260,000** for the 100 kg/day model, **<0.5 L/kg** water makeup, and **$0.80–1.80/kg** operating cost before material credits.

## Phase 5 — Ubiquity (2043–2050)

Make certified safe PCB recovery available within one day's logistics of nearly every large population center and through shared services for islands and rural regions. Pair machine-readable board passports and design-for-release standards with repair-first routing and public recovery accounting. Ubiquity means access and verified outcomes—not a fixed machine count: **>95% of collected PCB mass** enters formal reuse or recovery, while open burning, uncontrolled acid leaching, and untracked residue export approach zero.
