# Coastal Lens Recharge Gate — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1** · Research concept; phase gates are proposed, not completed.

## Concept

Define eligible roof, aquifer, flood and land-rights criteria with local operators, hydrogeologists and regulators. Complete a water-balance and groundwater-mounding model for the 100 m² roof, 5 m³ tank and open-overflow design; advance only if an approved source-water and sampling plan, safe bypass route and independent safe-water supply exist.

## Prototype

Build one inspectable HDPE tank, first-flush assembly, captive valve and accessible gallery mock-up. Bench-test 5 m³ gravity drawdown, overflow at the locally specified design storm, isolation under simulated contaminated inflow and maintenance without pressurizing soil; stop if any valve state can send rejected water to the gallery or unsafe flow to neighbors.

## Pilot

With permits, establish baselines and operate non-potable trials at 20 eligible sites for two wet seasons, paired with 20 hydrogeologically matched conventional MAR controls. Record admitted/bypassed volumes, head, EC, lab chloride, microbiology and nearby pumping; an independent review advances only if the pre-registered ≥10% dry-season inland chloride difference is robust and there is no increased chemical/microbial exceedance, neighbor-well harm or protected-flow loss.

## Scale

If the pilot passes, seek replicated trials across distinct coastal aquifers and price the complete installation, lab tests, service and replacement with local suppliers. Release open service procedures and site-specific operating limits only where regulators accept the evidence; halt expansion if benefits fail replication or full-life costs exceed established alternatives.

## Ubiquity

By 2050, offer a voluntary, auditable recharge option to qualified coastal utilities rather than a universal installation mandate. Maintain independent potable-water tests and pumping limits; continue only where annual public reports show sustained chloride benefit, acceptable water quality, secured maintenance funding and no displaced flood or water-rights harm.
