# Cookware Leach Verification Station — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** These are research gates, not delivery dates or proof of safety. Each phase depends on the previous one; failure to resolve false negatives ends the field-screening claim.

**Concept →** Define the eligible metal cookware, lead-only measurand, 4% acetic-acid 15-minute simmer plus 24-hour contact protocol, blank and uncertainty rules with a public-health partner. Compare at least 100 varied vessel extracts against an accredited ICP-MS laboratory, including worn pots and repeat cycles. Gate: a reproducible referral threshold and full-method reporting limit below that threshold; otherwise restrict the product to sample collection and lab referral.

**Prototype →** Build a contained heater/transfer fixture and offline bismuth-film ASV reader; test lot-to-lot electrodes, blind blanks, standard additions and spill interlocks. Gate: demonstrate a ≤2 µg/L full-method Pb reporting limit and ≤10-minute analysis-after-extraction target on relevant real matrices, with pre-registered recovery and invalid-run criteria; if those targets fail, revise the design rather than claim a negative screen.

**Pilot →** Place supervised units with a cookware-exchange partner and a foundry, compare at least 300 varied pots and both extraction time points with blinded accredited ICP-MS, and track repeatability, false negatives, invalids, total cost and replacement uptake. Gate: report the lower confidence bound around the proposed ≥95% sensitivity at the provisional 5 µg/L triage level, plus observed failure and referral rates; do not scale without regulator and partner review of residual risk.

**Scale →** If the pilot supports field decisions, qualify local electrode and reagent suppliers, waste collection, training, spare parts and periodic independent laboratory audits. Gate: reproducible performance across sites and lots, published lifecycle and per-pot costs including labor and confirmation, and a jurisdiction-approved screening/referral policy; the US$280 station and US$1.20 consumable goals remain unverified until supplier quotations and production runs support them.

**Ubiquity →** Conditional long-term adoption integrates random batch audits with market exchange services and feedstock traceability, not a universal safety sticker. Gate: independent surveillance shows durable removal of confirmed leaching cookware and improved exposure outcomes relative to comparable communities; retire or redesign the station if false reassurance, waste costs or superior laboratory access outweigh its benefits.
