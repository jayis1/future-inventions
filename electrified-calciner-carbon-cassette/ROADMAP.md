# Electrified Calciner Carbon Cassette — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1** · Concept-stage gates, not committed delivery dates. Compare every phase against the same site's direct-fired line and independent product/safety specifications.

## 1. Concept

Model the meal and CO₂ mass balance, hot-face heat transfer, seal leakage and grid duty for a named dry-process host; assay representative raw meal and unreturned kiln dust. Exit only with a peer-reviewed engineering safety case, storage route and an independently checkable baseline; the principal risk is that indirect channels cannot deliver the required heat at viable throughput.

## 2. Prototype

Build a guarded bench channel with replaceable high-alumina liner, FeCrAl heater, feed/discharge locks and instrumented gas outlet. Cycle real meal across normal and off-design compositions; qualify conversion, particulate loading, seal integrity and material wear against a control. Exit with repeatable balances and safe trip/relief tests; reject the design if the hot face or seals fail under cycling.

## 3. Pilot

Operate a bypassable host-plant slipstream over startup, steady state, planned maintenance and power/compressor interruptions. Require ≥90% *cassette-boundary* process-CO₂ collection across a defined full cycle, matched clinker quality, independent CO₂ metering, permitted storage custody and a positive measured net lifecycle benefit; otherwise pause, redesign or retire it. The risk is downtime or insufficient heat-transfer area at real throughput.

## 4. Scale

Replicate at multiple site-qualified lines only after an independent safety and emissions audit; test the aspirational ≥8,000 operating hours/year alongside itemized electricity, grid-connection, replacement, shutdown and storage costs. Publish comparable results and uncertainties, not just outlet purity; the risk is that storage logistics or electrical capacity dominate the economics.

## 5. Ubiquity

By 2050, consider wide licensing only where low-carbon power, trained maintenance, qualified clinker and verified durable storage coexist; continuously audit venting and retire sites that turn net-negative. This is a conditional scenario rather than a deployment forecast, and lower-clinker material choices remain complementary.
