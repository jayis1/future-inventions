## 3. Climate Impact

### 3.1 N₂O Abatement (Primary Climate Lever)

- N₂O GWP: 298× CO₂ (100-yr)
- Passive woodchip reactor N₂O emission: 3–15% of influent N
- EDDA N₂O emission: <0.3% of influent N
- **Abatement per ha:** 1.5–7.5 g N₂O/m³ × 20 m³/day × 365 = 11–55 kg N₂O/ha/yr avoided
- **CO₂-eq per ha:** 3.3–16.4 t CO₂-eq/ha/yr avoided
- **Global (120M ha):** 90–240 Mt CO₂-eq/yr (primary estimate in §2.4)

### 3.2 Displaced Haber–Bosch Ammonia

- Each tonne NH₃ ≈ 1.9 t CO₂ (natural gas reforming + compression)
- 3–9 Mt NH₃/yr displaced → **5.7–17.1 Mt CO₂/yr** avoided
- Additional energy redirect: 0.03–0.09% of global energy freed

### 3.3 Total Climate Impact

| Lever | CO₂-eq abated (global, 120M ha) |
|---|---|
| N₂O suppression | 84–223 Mt/yr |
| Displaced Haber–Bosch | 6–17 Mt/yr |
| **Total** | **90–240 Mt CO₂-eq/yr** |

For context: 90–240 Mt CO₂-eq/yr ≈ **0.2–0.5% of global GHG emissions** (~52 Gt CO₂-eq/yr) — from a single edge-of-field intervention on cropland drainage.

## 4. Ocean & Ecosystem Impact

| Dead Zone | Current Area | N Load Reduction with EDDA | Projected Area Reduction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gulf of Mexico | 22,700 km² | 60–90% of agricultural N flux | <5,000 km² (international target) |
| Baltic Sea | 70,000 km² | 50–70% of agricultural N flux | 20,000–35,000 km² |
| East China Sea | ~27,000 km² | 40–60% of agricultural N flux | 10,000–16,000 km² |
| Bay of Bengal | Growing | 30–50% of agricultural N flux | Stabilization + partial recovery |

**Ecosystem recovery timeline:** Benthic communities begin recolonizing within 2–5 years of oxygen restoration; fisheries productivity recovers over 5–15 years (Diaz & Rosenberg 2008).

## 5. Public Health Impact

| Population | Current Exposure | With EDDA at Scale |
|---|---|---|
| US Midwest rural well users | 1–10 mg N/L typical, >MCL in hotspots | <5 mg N/L across deployed regions |
| Northern European well users | 2–25 mg N/L in sandy aquifers (DK, NL) | <10 mg N/L |
| South Asian groundwater users | 10–50+ mg N/L in many wells (India, Bangladesh) | 30–60% reduction |

**Disease burden reduction (qualitative):**
- Methemoglobinemia ("blue baby syndrome"): near-elimination in deployed regions
- Colorectal cancer: 5–15% reduction in nitrate-exposed populations (per epidemiological dose-response)
- Thyroid dysfunction: measurable reduction in chronic exposure cohorts

### 6.2 Public-Good Economics

| Externality | Value per module/yr | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| CO₂-eq abated | $30–80/yr | 1.5–4.0 t CO₂-eq × $20/t |

| Drinking water treatment avoided | $20–60/yr | Marginal municipal treatment cost |
| Fishery recovery value | $10–50/yr | Proportional share of coastal fishery value |
| **Total externality value** | **$140–440/yr per module** | Exceeds OpEx by 2–10× |

### 6.3 Global Market

- Total addressable market: 120M ha × 1 module/ha = 120M modules
- At $1,500/module (scale): **$180B cumulative CapEx** over 20-yr deployment
- Annual fertilizer recovery market: 1.5–6.0 Mt (NH₄)₂SO₄ × $300/t = **$0.5–1.8B/yr**
- N-removal credit market potential: 1.5–2.7 Mt N/yr × $4–10/kg = **$6–27B/yr**

## 7. Comparison: EDDA vs. Status Quo

| Metric | Status Quo (passive + no action) | EDDA at Scale (120M ha) |
|---|---|---|
| Coastal dead zone area | 245,000 km² (growing) | 50,000–120,000 km² (shrinking) |
| Agricultural N₂O emissions | ~6–7 Mt N₂O/yr (~1.8 Gt CO₂-eq) | 1.8–3.0 Mt N₂O/yr (~540–900 Mt CO₂-eq) |
| Fresh ammonia for lost N | 8–12 Mt NH₃/yr | 3–9 Mt NH₃/yr displaced |
| Rural drinking water nitrate | Exceeds MCL in thousands of wells | Below MCL across deployed regions |
| Edge-of-field N removal | 25–60% (passive, seasonal) | 85–98% (year-round) |
