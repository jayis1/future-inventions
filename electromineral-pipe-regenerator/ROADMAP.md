# Roadmap — Electromineral Pipe Regenerator

## Phase 1: Concept
Validate the core electro-mineralization chemistry in benchtop lead-copper-galvanized pipe coupons using realistic drinking-water chemistries. The goal is to show repeatable formation of a dense hydroxyapatite-silica barrier, greater than 90% suppression of lead release versus untreated controls, and stable inline anodic stripping voltammetry readings at low-ppb concentrations.

## Phase 2: Prototype
Build a mobile service-skid prototype with recirculation pump, dosing manifold, pulsed DC control, MnO2 polishing cell, and automated verification workflow. This phase should prove treatment can be completed in a same-day service window for a single-family home or a small branch network, while safely capturing released lead into a sealed concentrate canister.

## Phase 3: Pilot
Deploy pilots in high-need settings such as schools, childcare centers, clinics, and older multifamily buildings across multiple water chemistries. Success means maintaining first-draw lead below 5 ppb at most treated outlets for at least 12 months, documenting operator workflow, and generating regulator-grade evidence that the system is a credible bridge to full pipe replacement.

## Phase 4: Scale
Industrialize the hardware into standardized home, campus, and municipal-response product tiers with certified consumables and regionally manufacturable modules. Utilities, remediation contractors, and public-health agencies begin using EPR as a rapid-response tool after corrosion-control failures, lead discoveries, or long replacement backlogs, supported by service software and chain-of-custody hazardous waste logistics.

## Phase 5: Ubiquity
Make active pipe rehabilitation as routine as water-heater maintenance. In this phase, permanent maintenance nodes, passive compliance reporting, and automated retreatment triggers become common in legacy buildings worldwide, reducing childhood lead exposure while replacement programs continue and eventually shrinking the global stock of untreated hazardous plumbing to a small residual edge case.
