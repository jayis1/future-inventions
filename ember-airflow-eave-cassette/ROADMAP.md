# Ember-Airflow Eave Cassette — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1** · Research concept; no phase has been completed. Each gate is conditional on the preceding evidence, not a calendar promise.

## Concept

Survey eligible boxed, noncombustible soffits and existing approved ember-resistant vents; freeze the 300 × 100 mm dual-barrier geometry, outward drain, cleaning access and matched-comparator test protocol. Gate: independent roof and fire specialists approve a test plan that measures installed NFVA, pressure-flow curves, ember entry, direct flame and rain, with explicit reject criteria.

## Prototype

Fabricate several 304 stainless/formed-steel variants and test clean, loaded, aged and deliberately mis-seated assemblies against a locally approved maintained vent in the same mock soffit. Gate: meet the README's fire, rain and airflow design targets simultaneously, retain the fixed barrier through cleaning, and reject geometry that cannot preserve required ventilation at realistic passive pressures.

## Pilot

After applicable third-party vent testing and local authority approval, monitor a permitted, small paired new-build/retrofit cohort with professional roof surveys, installation QA, baseline moisture readings and recurring drain, corrosion, blockage and pressure checks. Gate: no unresolved moisture or fire-safety failure and a defensible comparison of maintenance burden and cost against approved vents; stop or redesign on any unsafe finding.

## Scale

Qualify regional metal fabricators, corrosion-compatible joints, replacement parts and an auditable installation/inspection checklist; obtain jurisdiction-specific acceptance before distribution. Gate: repeat third-party and field performance on production lots and demonstrate a credible installed cost including survey, labor, maintenance and end-of-life recovery; the US$32 ex-workshop target alone is insufficient.

## Ubiquity

Conditionally integrate repairable eave inlets into broader roof-and-near-home wildfire hardening only where long-term field evidence supports both ember exclusion and attic moisture safety. Gate: publish multi-climate outcome data and independent reviews before claiming reduced structure losses; keep roofs, decks, windows and fuels in the resilience program rather than treating the cassette as a complete solution.
