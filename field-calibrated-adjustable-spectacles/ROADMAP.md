# Roadmap — Field-Calibrated Adjustable Spectacles

**Author: jayis1.** These are proposed stage gates, not dates of achieved development. Adult, predominantly spherical distance correction is the initial intended use; children and near-vision-only users require separate protocols.

## Concept

Specify the four-element polycarbonate Alvarez optical prescription, adjustable frame, paper-card fitting kit and referral rules. Compare local fixed-power alternatives and confirm that the signed power curve, lens aperture and assembly tolerances can plausibly meet the README's −4 to +3 D design range. Gate: independent optical review, preliminary bill of materials and a documented adult-use risk analysis; stop if the model offers no credible advantage over conventional supply.

## Prototype

Mould and coat optical elements, assemble captive screws and reversible locks, then measure centre and off-axis power, transmission, distortion, centration, impact behaviour and power drift over 1,000 adjustment cycles and environmental conditioning. Gate: reproducible ≤0.25 D indicated steps and ≤0.25 D post-conditioning lock drift as *bench targets*, not assumed results; revise optics or halt if safety or image quality fails.

## Pilot

With clinical oversight and local ethics/regulatory review, prospectively compare worker-assisted fittings for several hundred eligible adults against masked professional refraction, best-corrected acuity and a conventional-glasses pathway. Report the 6/12 binocular-acuity fraction, false-clear referral rate with confidence intervals, astigmatism exclusions, adverse events, time to fit and six-month wear. Gate: predefined ≥80% acuity and ≤5% false-clear targets plus regulator-approved intended use and referral pathway; do not infer pediatric safety from an adult trial.

## Scale

If the pilot passes, qualify coated moulding, optical inspection, spare-shield supply and local repair through an independently selected eye-care social enterprise or public procurement partner; no partnership is presumed. Gate: verify factory US$18–25/pair at 100,000 pairs/year *and* auditable delivered cost per sustained wearer, with funded referral completion and surveillance of damaged or drifting lenses. Compare bids against standard glasses and stop expansion where the latter work better.

## Ubiquity

By 2050, conditionally offer same-day adult screening and reversible spherical fitting through community eye-care networks while conventional prescriptions, complex refraction and disease care remain clinician-led. Gate: independently measured sustained wear, equitable referral completion, optical safety and lower total cost per adequately corrected user across settings; withdraw or narrow use wherever these measures worsen. This is a scenario, not a forecast.
