# Greywater Heat-Loop Cassette — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** All dates and specifications are planning targets, not accomplished milestones. No potable connection is permitted before local approval.

## Concept

Finalize the drain-side-only removable insert, stationary double-wall potable exchanger, visible vented telltale and gravity bypass around an existing trap. Exit gate: a qualified plumbing reviewer signs off a test plan for cross-leakage, overflow, stagnation and applicable local code pathways, with an installed-cost estimate for eligible service bays.

## Prototype

Build bench units using 316L drain-side surfaces and approved potable materials. Compare them with no recovery and an approved vertical DWHR unit at matched 9 L/min flows, 35°C drain and 15°C mains; repeat after hair/soap fouling and cleaning. Exit gate: ≥3°C steady drain cooling, <20 mm added drain head, <10-minute drain-only service, visible inter-wall leak indication and no overflow under deliberate blockage; all remain unverified until measured.

## Pilot

After third-party plumbing approval, instrument 30 matched installations for 12 months without retaining occupant identities or schedules. Exit gate: no cross-connections or blocked-drain floods, and independently audited net heater-input savings greater than cleaning energy/labor costs relative to controls; suspend deployment on any safety failure.

## Scale

Obtain applicable third-party certification and installer training, validate service intervals and component replacement, then solicit production quotes at 10,000 units/year. Exit gate: certified, repeatable production and independently costed installed payback within product life at the target market's actual tariff; the US$180 ex-factory figure remains a goal, not a quote.

## Ubiquity

Offer service-accessible, code-approved drain-heat recovery as an optional standard detail in new shower bays where measured inlet temperatures and usage justify it, while retaining conventional units elsewhere. Exit gate: multi-region field evidence that lifetime net energy and cost benefits survive maintenance, replacements and safe end-of-life recycling; adoption remains conditional, not assured.
