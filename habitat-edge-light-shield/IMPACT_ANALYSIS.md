# Habitat-Edge Light Shield — Impact Analysis

**Author: jayis1.** Scenario model, not a measured result or forecast. Its unit is one *eligible, approved luminaire retrofit*, not a hectare restored or an insect saved. All values are nominal US dollars; no discounting, taxes, certification, fixture replacement, monitoring, disposal or energy tariff is included. The 50% light-spill and 30% insect-observation figures are unvalidated design/pilot targets from the [README](README.md), not ecological outcome estimates.

## Inputs and method

| Assumption | Value | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parts per approved compatible luminaire | US$65 ex-factory at 10,000/year by 2038 | Unquoted volume target; not assured at other volumes |
| Survey, qualified installation and acceptance photometry | US$90–180 per site | Planning allowance; assumes one retrofit per site |
| Inspection/replacement | US$10–25 per luminaire-year | Planning allowance |
| Accounting horizon | Five years after installation | Excludes discounting and future requalification |
| Boundary vertical photopic illuminance | ≥50% below same unchanged fixture at mapped test points | Conditional acceptance target, not a measured mean |
| Road-light input power | At most 110% of unchanged compliant LED installation | Upper bound, not an energy saving |
| Flying insects at boundary | Pilot hypothesis: 30% fewer observations than matched unchanged control | Separate taxon-specific field endpoint; not attributable population change |

**Calculation:** initial installed cost = eligible retrofits × (US$65 parts + per-site survey/install cost); five-year gross cost = initial installed cost + eligible retrofits × five annual maintenance allowances. No assumption that all surveyed poles qualify. At any accepted boundary point with baseline illuminance B lux, the engineering target is ≤0.5B lux; B must be measured locally. A 40-treated-luminaire pilot needs matched controls and does not count the controls as retrofits.

## Conditional deployment scenarios

| Scenario | Accepted installations | Survey/install per site | Maintenance per unit-year | Initial gross cost | Five-year gross cost |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Low / constrained eligibility | 1,000 | US$180 | US$25 | US$245,000 | US$370,000 |
| Base / initial ceiling | 10,000 | US$135 | US$18 | US$2,000,000 | US$2,900,000 |
| High / post-scale sensitivity only | 50,000 | US$90 | US$10 | US$7,750,000 | US$10,250,000 |

The high case exceeds the README's **initial 10,000-installation ceiling** and is a sensitivity case contingent on later evidence and approvals, not a 2038 commitment. The US$65 volume price is held constant in this arithmetic solely to isolate adoption and service-cost sensitivity; at 1,000 units it is particularly uncertain. Multiply the measured site-specific accepted-boundary readings by neither the installation count nor an assumed habitat area: lux at selected points cannot establish hectares protected. A notional 50% point reduction at all qualifying sites means 1,000, 10,000 or 50,000 *treated light points*, not an equal number of independent ecological benefits.

## Benefits, counterfactuals and falsification

There is **no energy or CO2 saving booked**: a passive optic adds no power but maintaining road visibility might require different output, up to the 10% input ceiling. Meter wattage before and after at equivalent road conditions, then calculate energy difference as (baseline W − retrofit W) × measured operating hours ÷ 1,000 kWh; carbon accounting additionally requires an appropriate local marginal emission factor and embodied impacts of parts and servicing. Compare gross costs above with a quoted, approved purpose-built shielded-luminaire replacement over the same five years before claiming economic savings; no replacement-price baseline is available here.

Reject the ecological proposition if replicated paired surveys show no meaningful taxon-specific improvement or displaced harm, even if boundary lux falls. Reject a host-family retrofit if its electrical certification, road visibility, uniformity, glare, ingress or retention cannot be independently cleared. Track reflected light, weather and seasonal habitat use: direct-light suppression is not proof of reduced skyglow, reproductive success or population recovery. Publish exclusions, negative findings and confidence intervals rather than projecting insect totals from one pilot percentage.
