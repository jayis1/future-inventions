# Humidity-Guarded Oxygen Hub — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** Concept milestones, not evidence of a certified product. Each phase depends on passing its exit gate; clinical use requires device certification and local approval.

## Concept

Map humidity, dust, outage and repair failures at staffed clinics; specify a maintained certified 5 L/min comparator, outlet-quality metrics, independent reserve source and safe service boundary. Exit when the hazard analysis, bill of materials and test protocol are reviewed with biomedical technicians and clinicians, with no unaddressed single-point loss of alarm and gas-quality monitoring.

## Prototype

Build a bench-only oil-free PSA demonstrator with a replaceable dryer/sieve cassette and independent outlet analyser. At 40 °C, 95% RH and simulated 2,000 m altitude, seek continuous 5 L/min at 90–96% O2, ≤350 W whole-system input and ≥4 h battery operation after ageing allowance; stress-test condensate, sensor drift, interrupted power and oxygen-compatible materials. Fail any gate that cannot be reproduced by independent testing.

## Pilot

After electrical, gas, cleaning and regulatory validation, conduct a supervised, preregistered 12-month comparison at a small number of staffed clinics with maintained certified concentrators and identical reserve arrangements. Log qualified oxygen-hours, outlet concentration/flow/pressure, repairs, cylinder fallback and adverse events; proceed only if safety is non-inferior and the predefined availability and service-cost improvements hold.

## Scale

If the pilot succeeds and local device approval is secured, qualify a traceable supply chain for keyed service modules, train regional technicians and audit spare-parts and reserve-cylinder availability. Expand procurement only where measured lifetime cost per qualified litre, including 2 kWh battery replacement, dryer consumables and optional PV, beats the maintained comparator without worsening supply interruptions.

## Ubiquity

By 2050, consider humidity-resistant serviceability a procurement requirement wherever comparative local evidence supports it; maintain post-market alarm, contamination, failure and repair registries. The exit condition is sustained independently audited qualified output and no climate-related oxygen interruption attributable to the device in participating sites—not a promise that all clinics or patients will benefit.
