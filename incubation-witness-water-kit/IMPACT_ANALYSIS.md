# Incubation-Witness Water Kit — Impact Analysis

**Author: jayis1**

**Status: scenario model, not observed impact or a water-safety claim.** Horizon: one operating year after a qualified pilot. No prototype, measured fault rate or health-outcome dataset exists.

## Model and assumptions

- One reusable incubator per participating site; one sealed assay per run. Assumed 2 runs/site/week × 52 weeks = 104 planned runs/site/year; this is a planning workload, not observed demand. A single-sample unit can accommodate this cadence if staffing and turnaround permit.
- Low/base/high *illustrative deployment sizes*: 20/100/1,000 sites; these are not forecasts or a share of the 2.1 billion people without safely managed water.[2]
- Planned runs = sites × 104. Completed runs may be fewer; valid reports = completed runs × (1 − invalid fraction). Illustrative invalid fractions 20%/10%/5% for low/base/high are sensitivity inputs, **not** measured failure rates. A valid report may still be positive or unrepresentative; it is not a safe-water certificate.
- Existing unquoted targets: US$160 ex-workshop per incubator, five-year life and provisional 0.18 kWh per 18-hour run, excluding warm-up.[README](README.md). Replacement, controls, samples, reagent, training, electricity tariffs, confirmation, transport and waste treatment are **not priced**; no total-cost claim follows from the device-only target.

| Illustrative case | Sites | Planned runs/year | Assumed invalid fraction | Illustrative valid reports/year if all runs complete | Device purchase at target | Annual device amortization over five years |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Low | 20 | 2,080 | 20% | 1,664 | US$3,200 | US$640 |
| Base | 100 | 10,400 | 10% | 9,360 | US$16,000 | US$3,200 |
| High | 1,000 | 104,000 | 5% | 98,800 | US$160,000 | US$32,000 |

At full planned use, provisional incubation-only electricity is 2,080 × 0.18 = 374.4 kWh/year in the low case, 1,872 kWh/year in the base case and 18,720 kWh/year in the high case. Actual energy must be metered; the same assumptions imply US$32/104 ≈ US$0.31 device amortization per planned run *before* all recurring costs. No avoided illness, deaths or pollution mass is estimated: a chain from detected invalid runs to changed protective action to measured exposure and health outcomes has not been established.

## What would make this decision-useful

The pilot must measure completed/invalid/positive/negative runs separately, including missed invalids and false-invalids against an independent thermal record; compare with a standard incubator using the same assay and qualified laboratory confirmation. Log operator time, warm-up energy, calibration, battery replacement, control frequency, reagent/storage losses, per-sample disposal and all-in cost. Pre-register analysis by site and ambient condition; a benefit claim needs a comparator and uncertainty intervals, not merely kit distribution or nominal tests. Actions after positive and invalid results remain the responsibility of local water authorities.
