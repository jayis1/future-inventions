# Incubation-Witness Water Kit — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1**

**Status: proposed gates, not completed milestones.** Progress is conditional on trained operators, approved assay instructions, independent review and local regulatory acceptance.

## Concept

Define the sample/assay workflow, temperature envelope and fail-closed validity rule with water laboratories and local authorities. Exit when a hazard analysis covers sampling, drift, power loss, interpretation, UV exposure and cultured-waste disposal, and the reference assay and jurisdiction are identified.

## Prototype

Build a repairable single-vessel station with two calibrated probes, local event log, latched invalid flag and shielded readout. Exit after thermal mapping against independent thermometry across load and ambient extremes and a blinded 300-run fault challenge that reports detected faults, misses and false-invalids; failure requires redesign, not relabeling.

## Pilot

Under laboratory supervision, compare the same commercial assay in this station and a conventional controlled incubator at 20 small-supply sites. Exit only with prespecified analytical agreement, documented operator errors, total per-test cost, safe disposal and an independent assessment of whether the witness reduces invalid negatives without unacceptable false-invalids.

## Scale

Seek jurisdiction-specific method acceptance and independently audited electrical, UV, assay-handling and manufacturing controls. Exit when a service network demonstrates repeatable calibration, reagent traceability, spare-part availability, training and disposal at the proposed volume; the US$160 ex-workshop figure remains a target until quoted.

## Ubiquity

Expand only where continued surveillance shows benefit over ordinary incubators and resources exist for reagents, confirmation and repair. Exit criteria are sustained documented validity, equitable access and locally accountable action on positive or invalid results; widespread adoption is an aspiration, not an assumed outcome.
