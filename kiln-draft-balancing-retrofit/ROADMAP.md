# Kiln Draft Balancing Retrofit — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1**

**Status: proposed development gates, not achieved milestones.** Every phase concerns only eligible existing induced-draft zigzag kilns; qualified engineers and site operators retain combustion-safety authority. Training and cleaning precede hardware.

## 1. Concept

Map pressure, temperature, fan load, CO and accessible transition channels at candidate kilns; compare training-only operation first. Design a removable ≥60% Al₂O₃ guide and mechanically opening bypass without changing the original exhaust path. **Exit:** an independent kiln engineer signs a site-specific hazard review and a test plan covering fan loss, blocked taps, guide fracture and occupied-opening CO; reject sites without safe access or sufficient exhaust capacity. Budget and duration are to be scoped from the survey, not promised today.

## 2. Prototype

Build one instrumented kit and bench-test its spring, damper travel, guide mounting and sensor cleaning over representative fouling and thermal cycles. At one approved kiln, install cold and characterize startup, steady firing, bypass and shutdown with independent CO alarms and existing interlocks intact. **Exit:** no unsafe positive pressure at occupied openings in the approved envelope and repeatable return toward the baseline resistance on each tested fault; stop and redesign after any safety-gate failure.

## 3. Pilot

Run one season at **20 matched kiln pairs**, all receiving the same stacking/firing training; fit the kit only in one arm, pre-register analysis and measure weighed coal, saleable bricks, fan kWh, stack PM₂.₅/CO, worker-area CO and rejects. **Exit target, not a result:** ≥5% less fuel and ≥10% less PM₂.₅ per saleable brick than training-only, with no increase in CO, worker exposure, fan electricity or rejects, and with uncertainty and absolute emissions reported. An independent test team audits bypass and missing-data intervals.

## 4. Scale

If the pilot passes, qualify local refractory makers and cold-shutdown technicians, publish installation exclusions and price the kit plus recurring maintenance; test financing with cluster buyers rather than assuming carbon-credit revenue. **Exit:** repeat the pilot gates across multiple kiln clusters and seasons while independently tracking failures, maintenance cost, warranty claims and the stated US$1,500 ex-factory volume-cost estimate. Do not expand to natural-draft kilns by analogy.

## 5. Ubiquity

Where still relevant after cleaner-kiln transitions, make documented flow diagnostics and serviceable guides an optional standard for eligible kilns, with local operators able to remove or repair parts safely. **Exit:** sustained independently verified fuel, emissions and safety performance across seasons, affordable local service and regulatory approval; retire the retrofit wherever training, a cleaner fuel or replacement kiln performs better. This is a conditional 2050 vision, not a market forecast.
