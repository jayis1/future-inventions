# Roadmap — Laundry Microfiber Capture Cassette

**Author:** jayis1
**Status:** proposed gates, not completed milestones. Advance only after independent safety and retention review; see [Specification](SPECIFICATION.md).

## Concept

Model the washer pump, standpipe air gap, lint loading and disposal pathway; select polypropylene, EPDM and woven stainless mesh only after chemical compatibility screening. Exit when an installer can define a measurable eligible washer/drain envelope, a dry-service procedure and a safe rejection criterion for incompatible installations.

## Prototype

Build replaceable 500 µm / 60 µm screens and a latched restriction/relief witness. Across 50 cycles per candidate washer type, measure clean and loaded pressure, discharge time, leaks and pump temperature, including misassembly and blocked-drain faults; reject any setup outside maker limits. In ≥30 randomized paired washes with blanks, seek ≥70% synthetic-fiber mass reduction for ≥100 µm fibers and <1% relief events under a defined service interval.

## Pilot

Run a 100-household, 12-month comparative study against no filter and a contemporary external filter. Independently audit pouch disposal, missed service, leaks, relief and actual maintenance time; stop for attributable pump damage or significant flooding. Exit only if intention-to-treat retention, safe drainage and a cradle-to-disposal assessment support net benefit rather than merely bench capture.

## Scale

Qualify more washer models and local plumbing rules, validate gasket/screen aging under hot detergent and bleach cycles, and obtain relevant appliance/plumbing approvals before broad sales. At a proposed 50,000 units/year, request supplier quotes against the $80 ex-factory design target and demonstrate affordable installation, spare parts, collection pouches and an auditable waste route; redesign if users cannot service it reliably.

## Ubiquity

Offer a documented compatibility list, repairable spare parts and installer training only where multi-year field evidence shows safe operation and sustained retention. Publish independent annual estimates of eligible-fiber capture, bypass and downstream disposal loss; keep unsupported washer types out and revise or withdraw the product if lifecycle burden or flooding risk outweighs benefit. The 2050 vision is conditional, not a deployment forecast.
