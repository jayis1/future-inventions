# Maternity Gas-Loss Witness — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1** · Concept only; every clinical-site step needs local medical-gas engineering and clinical approval. Phases are evidence gates, not promised dates.

## Concept

Map cylinder turnover, clinical-demand accounting and authorised test boundaries at candidate maternity sites. Agree an uncertainty budget and compare a cart against the simpler alternative of an existing engineer-led audit or point-of-use supply. Exit only with a documented safe isolation plan, approved test medium and a falsifiable bench protocol.

## Prototype

Build a qualified, test-only pressure head, two-channel logger and scale interface. Challenge sealed blanks and 0.05–0.2 standard L/min references across relevant volumes, pressure and temperature; evaluate the proposed 0.1 standard L/min in 100 L over 20 min and <0.01 standard L/min test-head blank. Exit only if the full uncertainty interval distinguishes the reference from blank without unsafe connections; publish misses, false calls and failure states.

## Pilot

With approvals and backup supply, perform supervised, blinded comparisons against independent qualified engineer-led audits at several sites. Time the section tests separately from shutdown and recommissioning; reconcile cylinder masses over a complete annual supply cycle and record any service interruption. Exit only if safety, localization and reconciliation are demonstrably useful compared with ordinary practice; do not infer patient outcomes or room-air exposure from pressure tests.

## Scale

Qualify gas-compatible materials, connectors, cleaning, metrology and service processes under each deployment jurisdiction's applicable requirements. Train authorised engineers and compare total cost—including labour, certification and downtime—to repair, decommissioning or portable-cylinder alternatives. Exit only after independent product/site approval, repeatable production checks and economically justified maintenance intervals.

## Ubiquity

Offer a locally serviceable audit method only where recurring measured pre-use loss makes it useful; do not put a cart in every facility by default. Publish anonymised annual reconciliation and repair outcomes with uncertainty and a documented retirement path for sites that eliminate the distribution problem. Continued use depends on verified benefit, safe practice and local review, not a projected global rollout.
