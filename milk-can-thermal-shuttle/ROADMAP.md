# Milk-Can Thermal Shuttle — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** Evidence-gated research plan; phase names do not imply a promised launch date. See [README](README.md) for the trial conditions and safety limits.

## Concept

Freeze the geometry, 20 L load, 12 kg sealed-ice budget and ≤8°C trial limit; model wall transfer and heat ingress at 35°C ambient. Exit only when a documented energy balance, sanitation workflow, ergonomic review and local dairy-rule gap assessment identify tests for every critical assumption.

## Prototype

Build washable split-shell and removable-spreader units with an offline logger. In repeated controlled runs, test 30°C → ≤8°C within 2 h and a further 3 h at or below 8°C, sampling the warmest milk location and metering each ice recharge. Inspect leaks, seal wear, drops and cleaning residue; redesign on any failed gate.

## Pilot

With regulatory and food-contact review, run a 100-farm, 60-day matched or randomized comparison against ordinary insulated cans under the same handling and ice availability. Record microbiology, rejection, complete temperature traces, kWh per accepted litre, labor and breakage; advance only if predefined safety and cost endpoints beat the comparator without hidden freezer subsidy.

## Scale

Qualify local suppliers for food-contact cans and replaceable shells, establish freezer uptime and calibration service, and audit batches across multiple seasons and routes. Require documented repair, recall, disposal and training procedures plus a lifecycle and total-cost result that remains viable under real failure rates before increasing production.

## Ubiquity

Offer interoperable pack exchange only where cooperatives can sustain refrigeration, clean water, trained intake and independent verification. Keep locally readable records and lot-quarantine authority even at large adoption; withdraw or revise the design wherever field surveillance or changed dairy rules show it cannot meet requirements.
