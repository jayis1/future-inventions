# Mill-Gate Maize Sorter — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1. All milestones are proposed gates, not achieved results.** Advance only after independent review of food safety, operator safety, and reject custody.

## Concept

Build a shielded benchtop four-lane optical rig and a reference set spanning local maize varieties, moisture and visible damage. Define a representative lot-sampling protocol and assay input, accepted and hold fractions independently. Exit only with repeatable optical and gate timing, a documented power-loss-to-hold test, and an honest estimate of whether the US$280 ex-factory 2038 target is feasible; otherwise redesign.

## Prototype

Package the 365 nm LED, paired emission photodiodes, reflected-light camera, offline controller and spring-return gates into a cleanable guarded unit. Test dry whole maize at the ≥20 kg/h and ≤15 W design points, log false acceptance, sound-kernel false rejection, cleaning time and gate failures, and challenge every interlock. Exit only if fault injection prevents accepted discharge and blinded assays justify advancing; otherwise stop or revise thresholds and mechanics.

## Pilot

Run a 12-month, multi-site comparison of sorter, hand sorting and no sorting on naturally contaminated, independently sampled local lots. Seek ≥50% reduction in total aflatoxin mass in accepted grain with ≤5% sound-kernel false rejection, while separately recording lot-limit failures and the real cost of assays and held-grain disposal. Exit only with reproducible benefit across varieties and seasons, audited hold custody, and no increase in false reassurance; a missed safety gate halts expansion.

## Scale

If pilot evidence supports it, commission repairable local assembly, supplier-quoted parts and technician training; negotiate a miller/cooperative lease that funds testing and lawful reject disposition rather than merely a US$280 factory price. Validate food-contact materials, dust/electrical guarding, warranty service and relevant local approvals. Exit when independent production audits sustain the pilot's safety and cost gates across multiple manufacturing batches.

## Ubiquity

Offer a standard pre-milling fitting only where independent lot testing, trained operators, traceable hold bins and affordable servicing are routine. Periodically revalidate against new varieties and seasons; aggregate anonymous lot-level signals for regional monitoring only with governance and quality checks. Widespread installation is conditional on measured exposure reduction and durable incentives—not a substitute for food regulation or a promise of toxin-free maize by 2050.
