## 11. Risks to Impact Realization

| Risk | Impact on timeline | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Gyrotron field reliability | +2–5 yr | Fusion-grade MTBF already >5,000 hr; surface-only electronics |
| Permitting (deep drilling + stimulation) | +3–8 yr | Existing Class V framework; FORGE traffic-light precedent |
| Public acceptance ("fracking" association) | +2–5 yr | Closed-loop, no chemicals, M<2, transparent data |
| First-of-kind cost overrun | +$0.5–1B | DOE loan guarantee; phased pilot → commercial |
| Reservoir thermal drawdown | -10–20% output over 40 yr | Closed-loop tuning; lateral extension to fresh hot rock |
| Supply chain (gyrotrons, Inconel) | +1–3 yr | Industrialize gyrotron production (fusion demand already scaling) |
