# Roadmap — Millimeter-Wave Deep Geothermal

> Five-phase development path from concept to planetary ubiquity.

---

## Phase 1 — Concept & Component Validation (2025–2027)

Establish the scientific and engineering basis for each subsystem independently. At MIT/PSFC and partner labs, demonstrate mm-wave rock vaporization at 50–200 kW on granite/basalt cores under simulated downhole pressure (up to 30 MPa) and temperature (up to 400 °C), validating the energy-balance drilling-rate model (target: ≥30 m/hr at 1.5 MW equivalent). Characterize corrugated waveguide loss at 28 GHz over 1–2 km spooled lengths (target: <0.5 dB/km). In parallel, run sCO₂ recompression Brayton loop tests at Sandia/NREL (50–500 kWe) confirming 30–40% efficiency at 250–350 °C inlet. Publish open-data borehole-integrity and induced-seismicity modeling. **Deliverable:** a peer-reviewed, experimentally-grounded feasibility case that mm-wave drilling + sCO₂ cycle can be integrated. **Budget: $20–50M** (DOE ARPA-E + private).

## Phase 2 — Prototype Drill + Shallow Reservoir (2027–2030)

Build a first-of-kind mobile gyrotron drilling rig (1.5 MW, 28 GHz) on a standard oilfield truck spread. Drill a 1–2 km borehole in hard crystalline rock at a non-geothermal site (e.g., Nevada, Cornwall, or Larderello), demonstrating 30–70 m/hr penetration, in-situ vitreous casing, and continuous waveguide payout — no tripping. Separately, drill a 3–5 km well at an existing EGS site (FORGE, Utah) and stimulate a small closed-loop sCO₂ reservoir (target: 5–10 kg/s, 200 °C+). Couple the reservoir to a 1–5 MWe sCO₂ pilot turbine. **Deliverable:** first electricity generated from an mm-wave-drilled, sCO₂-cycled superhot-rock reservoir. **Budget: $150–300M** (DOE + venture + strategic investors).

## Phase 3 — Pilot Power Plant (2030–2034)

Deploy the first integrated 10–20 MWe MWDG power plant at a site with known superhot rock at 3–5 km depth. Demonstrate 90%+ capacity factor over 12+ months of continuous operation, 30–40% cycle efficiency, <M2 induced seismicity under a published traffic-light protocol, and near-zero water use. Validate LCOE components against model ($0.06–0.10/kWh first-of-kind, with a clear path to $0.03–0.06 at scale). Co-locate a small thermal desalination or district-heat cogen stream to demonstrate multi-output value. Publish open microseismic and reservoir-thermal-drawdown data. **Deliverable:** a bankable, permitted, operating reference plant that insurers, utilities, and regulators can inspect. **Budget: $400–800M** (project finance + DOE loan guarantee).

## Phase 4 — Commercial Scale-Up (2034–2042)

Factory-build standardized 50 MWe modules (gyrotron rig, sCO₂ turbine skid, dry cooler) at $700–1,200/kW. Deploy 500–2,000 plants globally, prioritizing coal-plant retrofits (reusing grid interconnect, land, workforce), island diesel displacement, and industrial process-heat clusters. Establish a global drilling-services industry (licensed rigs operating at $150–600/m). Drive LCOE to $0.03–0.05/kWh. Develop a carbon-credit pathway for the sCO₂ mineral-trapping co-benefit. Build a trained workforce (drilling engineers, reservoir modelers, turbine technicians) — target 200K+ jobs. **Deliverable:** MWDG is the default new-build firm clean power source, displacing new gas and coal. **Budget: $30–80B** (private project finance, self-funding from revenue).

## Phase 5 — Ubiquity & the 2050 Backbone (2042–2050)

Scale to 30,000–50,000 plants totaling 1.5–2.5 TW, supplying 40–70% of global electricity as firm clean baseload. MWDG plants sited in every continent (including water-stressed regions, islands, and industrial parks), enabling 95–100% clean grids by firming solar/wind without storage overbuild. 5–10 Gt CO₂/yr avoided; the gas-peaker fleet retired; 2.4B+ people gain reliable fuel-independent electricity. Industrial process heat (steel, cement, chemicals) largely decarbonized by direct superhot-rock heat. The gyrotron, once a fusion curiosity, is the planet's universal drilling tool. Deep rock — humanity's largest, cleanest, most democratic energy endowment — is finally within reach. **Cumulative investment: $1.5–3T** (a fraction of annual fossil-fuel capex).

---

*Milestones are indicative; actual timelines depend on permitting reform, capital availability, and public acceptance. Each phase is gated by the prior phase's demonstrated safety, cost, and performance.*
