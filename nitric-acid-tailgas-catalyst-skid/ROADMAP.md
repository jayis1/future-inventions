# Roadmap — Nitric-Acid Tailgas Catalyst Skid

**Author: jayis1** · Research concept; each phase requires an independent go/no-go review.

## Concept

Map the absorber-to-expander pressure, temperature and wet-gas composition on candidate trains; compare existing abatement bids and run a process-hazard screen. Exit only with at least one credible site envelope, an auditable N₂O mass balance and a written test plan for reductant-free Fe-zeolite activity versus a separately controlled reductant option.

## Prototype

Expose keyed monolith cartridges to representative NOₓ, oxygen, moisture and transients in a contained test loop. Record N₂O conversion, NOₓ byproducts, pressure loss and material aging; proceed only if repeat runs support the proposed ≤300 Pa and ≥90% targets at the proposed flow/temperature envelope without unsafe chemistry.

## Pilot

Install a permitted, mechanically certified skid on a consenting plant after a site hazard study and outage plan. Operate for 12 months with calibrated inlet/outlet flow and N₂O, independent stack tests and complete bypass/downtime logs; proceed only if online mass-weighted abatement over the year meets ≥90%, total annual reduction including outages is disclosed, permit limits hold and net energy/cost comparisons remain credible.

## Scale

Qualify repeatable cartridge manufacture, vessel certification, supply and spent-catalyst handling across multiple nonidentical eligible trains. Exit only after independent replication at three sites, documented catalyst aging and installed-cost/outage benchmarking against commercial abatement alternatives; reject sites requiring uncompetitive supplemental heating.

## Ubiquity

Offer a certified module and open measurement protocol wherever the train-specific envelope and permits support them; this is a 2050 scenario, not a market forecast. Sustain adoption only with audited multi-year emissions, compliant NOₓ control, published remaining stack emissions and periodic replacement/requalification, rather than assuming every nitric-acid plant qualifies.
