# Roadmap: Ocean Alkalinity Restoration Array (OARA)

> Five-phase development path from concept to global ubiquity.

---

## Phase 1: Concept & Lab Validation (2024–2027)

Define system architecture, validate core chemistry in laboratory settings. Build bench-scale BMED + rock weathering reactor (1–10 L/min seawater throughput) to demonstrate the "double alkalinity" synergy — confirming that acid-stream mineral dissolution yields 2–3× the alkalinity of base-stream-only approaches. Characterize membrane longevity under real seawater conditions (biofouling, scaling), test candidate basalt/olivine feedstocks for dissolution kinetics and trace-metal release, and model platform-scale alkalinity dispersal using high-resolution ocean circulation models (MITgcm, ROMS). Publish peer-reviewed validation studies. Secure initial IP patents on the integrated BMED + weathering reactor design. **Target:** TRL 3–4, lab-demonstrated synergy, $5–10M funding (government grants + climate-tech VC).

---

## Phase 2: Prototype Platform (2027–2030)

Build and sea-trial the first full-scale OARA prototype — a single 20-ft-equivalent platform deployed in controlled coastal waters (e.g., Monterey Bay or Bermuda Testbed Mooring). Integrate wave energy converter, BMED stack, rock weathering reactor, H₂ capture, sensor suite, and autonomous control. Demonstrate 30–90 days of continuous unattended operation with real-time MRV data streaming. Validate CO₂ removal accounting via paired upstream/downstream alkalinity + pCO₂ measurements. Iterate on membrane anti-fouling protocols, diffuser design for safe alkalinity dispersal, and storm-survival mode. **Target:** TRL 5, 1 prototype at sea, 50–200 t CO₂/yr demonstrated, $20–50M funding (Series A + DOE/ARPA-E grants).

---

## Phase 3: Pilot Fleet (2030–2035)

Deploy **1,000 platforms** in 3–5 target zones: Great Barrier Reef, Coral Triangle, Pacific Northwest shellfish coast, and Galicia rías. Each zone gets 100–300 platforms with密集 MRV sensor arrays and independent scientific oversight. Demonstrate measurable local pH restoration (+0.05–0.2 pH units) and aragonite saturation improvement in reef zones within 2–3 years. Establish the carbon credit methodology — get OARA-based OAE credits certified under a major standard (Verra, Gold Standard, or new OAE-specific protocol). Begin commercial H₂ offload contracts with coastal hydrogen offtakers. Refine manufacturing for cost reduction toward $100K/unit. **Target:** TRL 6–7, 1,000 platforms, 50–200 Kt CO₂/yr removed, $200–500M funding (Series B/C + carbon credit pre-purchases).

---

## Phase 4: Scale-Up (2035–2045)

Ramp production to **100,000 platforms** via automated manufacturing (target: 50–100 units/day at $50–80K/unit). Deploy across all major subtropical gyres and expanded reef rescue zones. Establish global service vessel fleet (50–100 autonomous service vessels for rock replenishment + H₂ offload). Achieve $50–100/t CO₂ net cost. Generate 5–20 Mt CO₂/yr in certified carbon credits — a meaningful fraction of the durable CDR market. Co-produce 50–200 Kt H₂/yr. Work with London Convention/Protocol to establish the international governance framework for open-ocean OAE at scale. Spin off regional operating companies (Pacific OARA, Atlantic OARA, Indian Ocean OARA) for localized deployment and maintenance. **Target:** TRL 8, 100,000 platforms, 5–20 Mt CO₂/yr, $2–5B cumulative investment, carbon-credit-financed scale-up.

---

## Phase 5: Ubiquity (2045–2060)

Reach **1,000,000 platforms** — a global fleet restoring ocean chemistry at planetary scale. Remove **0.4–1.6 Gt CO₂/yr** permanently, offsetting 0.05–0.1 pH units of global ocean acidification. Coral reef zones maintain Ω_arag > 3.0; shellfish industries worldwide recovered. Co-produce 1–2 Mt H₂/yr. The OARA becomes infrastructure — as ubiquitous and unremarkable as offshore wind farms or shipping lanes. Manufacturing cost falls to $30–50K/unit via mass production. The platform design is open-sourced, enabling any coastal nation to manufacture and deploy their own fleets. Ocean alkalinity restoration is recognized as a permanent public service, financed by carbon markets and green hydrogen revenue, governed under an international Ocean Chemistry Restoration Treaty. The ocean's pH stabilizes and begins recovering toward pre-industrial levels. **Target:** TRL 9, 1M platforms, 0.4–1.6 Gt CO₂/yr, $20–60B cumulative investment, self-sustaining via carbon credits + H₂ revenue.
