# Roadmap — Offline Care Consent Card

**Author: jayis1**

**Status:** Conditional research plan; no prototype or clinical validation yet. Each gate requires a paper-record comparator and a non-card care pathway.

## Concept

Specify the minimal field set, consent semantics, reader trust model, accessible fallback and threat model with patients, clinicians and privacy/legal reviewers. Exit only when the request/approval flow and a locally acceptable recordkeeping policy are documented; redesign if a card press could be mistaken for treatment consent or the data cannot be minimized.

## Prototype

Build a small test batch of NFC-powered cards and offline readers, then measure energy under weak coupling, switch faults, malformed inputs, revocation lag and read success after handling. Run multilingual usability sessions with consenting adults and accessible alternatives. Exit on reproducible scoped disclosure and readable failure states; stop if the display, switch or cryptographic flow cannot operate reliably within the power budget.

## Pilot

After local ethics, privacy, legal and clinical review, compare voluntary use with paper and existing offline workflows at the proposed 20-clinic, 10,000-cardholder scale only if earlier safety gates pass. Pre-register comprehension, read success, identity errors, unauthorized disclosures, lost-card burden and staff time. Stop or redesign on elevated clinical error, exclusion or coercive use; issuance alone is not success.

## Scale

If independent comparison shows net benefit, qualify suppliers, establish issuer-key governance, multilingual training, replacement/recycling and interoperability contracts; expand in stages with incident reporting and audit. Exit only if total cost and equitable access remain acceptable across multiple care settings; pause procurement when reader compromise or support burden exceeds the comparator.

## Ubiquity

Offer the card as one optional portable-record pathway alongside paper and local software, with continuing evaluation of refusal rights, accessibility and revocation gaps. The exit condition is sustained demonstrated benefit under independent oversight, not a numeric adoption quota; retire it where simpler alternatives work better.
