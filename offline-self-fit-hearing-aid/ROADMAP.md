# Offline Self-Fit Hearing Aid — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1. Concept only; milestones are gates, not completed results.**

## Concept

Freeze the adult mild-to-moderate use case, non-radio four-band design and physical-controls fitting flow. Review FDA OTC requirements and local rules with hearing-care and regulatory specialists; recruit users with diverse languages, dexterity and literacy for formative design. Exit only with documented hazard controls, a realistic US$65 ex-factory cost model at the proposed 100,000-aid/year scale, and a defined referral pathway for red-flag symptoms.

## Prototype

Build bench units with the 0.5 Wh removable LiFePO4 cell, ear-simulator-calibrated output, bounded gain, fault-triggered mute and power-loss-safe profile storage. Independently measure output, distortion, latency (target below 10 ms), feedback stability and aged-cell endurance (target at least 12 hours); abuse-test charging and removable-cell contacts. Exit only when independent acoustic and electrical safety review permits a supervised human study; a failed fitting must not silently increase gain.

## Pilot

Under required ethics and regulatory authorization, compare self-fitting with audiologist fitting in eligible adults using real-ear response, speech-in-noise recognition, user-reported communication, usability and adverse events. Track all aborted fits, referrals and six-month continued use (target at least 80%, not an observed result), with a one-month check and six-month follow-up. Do not advance on lab gain curves alone; require a prespecified safety and benefit review against a locally available comparator.

## Scale

Only after the applicable market authorizations and reproducible pilot outcomes, qualify multiple suppliers, calibrate every finished aid and set up local assembly, repair, safe cell recycling and stocks of domes and guards. Audit the US$65 ex-factory and US$5/aid-year consumables assumptions with invoices and field returns; expand distribution only where ear-care referral and service capacity keep pace with units shipped.

## Ubiquity

Make phone-free fitting and accessible repair available through community services and retail while preserving clinical routes, captions and sign language as alternatives. Sustain independently audited acoustic limits, retained-use measurement, transparent complaints and periodic affordability checks across regions. This phase is conditional on durable safety, real communication benefit and functioning follow-up—not a date or a promise of universal adoption.
