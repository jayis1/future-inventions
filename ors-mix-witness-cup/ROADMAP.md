# ORS Mix-Witness Cup — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** Proposed research gates, not completed milestones. Standard label-based ORS preparation and access to care remain available at every phase.

## Concept

Specify the single supported one-litre packet and source-water envelope; prototype conductivity and refractive-index measurement on blinded laboratory mixes across 10–40 °C, three mineralization levels and multiple packet lots. Exit only if the two signals add measurable discrimination over a marked vessel alone without plausibly passing unsafe substitution challenges; otherwise stop or simplify to a passive vessel.

## Prototype

Build cleanable polypropylene vessels and removable test channels with non-color-only feedback, electrical/optical reference checks and fail-invalid behavior. Compare mixed aliquots with laboratory sodium, glucose and osmolality assays; target ≥90% detection of half-/double-strength challenges and ≤1% false passes with prespecified confidence bounds, plus fill error within ±20 mL and read time ≤15 s. Exit only after cleaning, food-contact migration and wear testing support a recalibration interval, and usability participants can interpret invalid status without improvising a correction.

## Pilot

After ethics and jurisdiction-specific regulatory review, compare the reader-plus-marked-vessel workflow against marked vessel and packet instructions in supervised community sites. Pre-register preparation-error rates, false passes, invalid rates, cleaning compliance, treatment delay, referrals and adverse events; exit only on independently reviewed evidence of net benefit with no clinically meaningful delay. Do not infer clinical efficacy from bench discrimination alone.

## Scale

Qualify lots, calibration records, repair and recycling channels, and a support plan for battery, replacement channel and staff training. Validate the unproven US$24 ex-factory target at 100,000 units/year against actual delivered and per-usable-check costs; expand only if the pilot's safety and cost advantage survives replication across water and packet supply chains.

## Ubiquity

By 2050, if evidence warrants it, make the witness an optional, maintainable checkpoint at preparation sites while preserving simple marked-container preparation everywhere. Publish surveillance of false passes, downtime, replacements and treatment delays; withdraw or redesign a model whose real-world performance falls below its validated envelope. Adoption is a contingent scenario, not a clinical or procurement forecast.
