# Owner-Fitted Deorbit Cassette — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1**
**Status: proposed gates, not completed milestones.** The evaluation envelope is 10–100 kg near-circular 550–700 km LEO, with a 200 m captive tape, 2 m² sail and ≤3 kg cassette. A five-year disposal target applies only to approved reference orbits and conservative-density cases; no flight performance has been established. See [README.md](README.md) for the mechanism and [IMPACT_ANALYSIS.md](IMPACT_ANALYSIS.md) for conditional fleet scenarios.

## Concept

Define owner-fit mechanical and safety interfaces, baseline sail-only against sequential tape/sail for identical host orbits, and propagate low-solar-activity lifetimes and integrated conjunction exposure. Include a preliminary hazard and reentry assessment, and reject mission classes where tape exposure or inadequate drag defeats the five-year target. **Exit:** a reviewed requirements set and bounded go/no-go envelope for each host and orbit, without assuming the two-stage variant is superior.

## Prototype

Build captive deployment articles and an independently powered, dual-inhibited controller. Measure release loads, tape circuit/current in representative plasma, thermal-vacuum and vibration durability, dormant-reserve life, safe non-activation and tumbling-area derating; model uncertainty from these observations. **Exit:** reproducible functional and non-activation tests with no released fragments, plus a documented failure analysis; these are not flight qualification.

## Pilot

With consenting operators and launch/regulatory approval, compare an equipped spacecraft with a matched unmodified baseline using independently tracked orbital decay; publish the orbit, solar activity, deviations and conjunction screening. Do not promise a controlled reentry. **Exit:** tracked deployment and an observed decay-rate change consistent with a revised propagation model, no unacceptable encounter/severance outcome, and an independent safety review before broader use.

## Scale

Qualify manufacturing and owner integration across the approved 10–100 kg host classes, including traceable release hardware, shelf-life checks and documented training; audit whether an ex-factory US$250 unit target is attainable only at the proposed 100,000/year rate, exclusive of integration and launch. **Exit:** repeatable acceptance testing and measured fleet reliability against the ≥99% deployment target, with regulators and operators accepting the specific installation envelopes.

## Ubiquity

Offer a disposal evidence package alongside every approved cassette, monitor tracking through reentry, and periodically retire configurations that underperform a simpler sail-only or other safe disposal option. The 2050 vision is broad *owner-installed* adoption, not universal certification. **Exit:** independently audited lifetime and encounter outcomes across solar cycles, transparent reliability data and ongoing governance of failures and reentry risk.
