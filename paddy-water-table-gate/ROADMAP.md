# Paddy Water-Table Gate — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1. All dates and thresholds are proposed exit gates, not accomplished milestones.** The design remains a research concept; compare it with an ordinary AWD observation tube and manual operation.

## Concept

Specify eligible 0.1 ha, level, gravity-fed fields; test inlet head, bund seal, silt loading and the −15 cm / +3–5 cm latch sequence in a flume. Exit only when independent ruler readings corroborate both floats and a safe overflow/upstream isolation arrangement is documented for candidate sites.

## Prototype

Build 50 repairable HDPE/PP units with EPDM seats and stainless pins/springs; run accelerated sediment, corrosion, linkage-break and dry-canal tests plus one cropping season in two soil types. Exit target: ±2 cm lower-trigger repeatability, upper stop at +3–5 cm and fewer than one missed or uncommanded refill per 100 eligible cycles, with faults and interventions logged.

## Pilot

Pair 100 gate-managed 0.1 ha plots with 100 manual-AWD plots across wet and dry seasons; include appropriate continuous-flooded reference plots. Meter supply, water table, yield, labor, CH₄ and N₂O and document grain-contaminant risks. Exit only if no more than 5% yield loss against manual AWD, fewer missed refill events and site-specific net benefit after servicing; no carbon-credit claim from the mechanical flag alone.

## Scale

If the pilot passes, qualify local assembly, replacement parts, bundled installation and field-repair training before a 1,000-unit multi-district release. Target US$35 ex-factory at 100,000 units/year only after audited costings; exit on independent multi-season service costs, leakage/fault records and verified benefits relative to manual AWD, not a projected carbon price.

## Ubiquity

By 2050, conditional on suitable irrigation control and independently replicated results, offer an interoperable bund fitting through farmer groups and irrigation districts rather than mandate it for rain-fed or high-percolation fields. The exit criterion is sustained farmer choice, repair access and independently measured water/yield/net-GHG outcomes; remove the product where manual AWD performs as well at lower total cost.
