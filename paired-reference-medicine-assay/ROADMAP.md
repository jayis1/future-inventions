# Paired-Reference Medicine Assay — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1**

**Status:** proposed research gates, not achieved milestones. No phase authorizes medicine release without applicable regulatory approval.

## Concept

Define one single-API, immediate-release tablet formulation and its sampling plan, qualified 80%/100% reference source, extraction solvent, chromatographic separation and HPLC adjudication method. Exit only after bench feasibility shows distinct, unsaturated bands and a monotonic optical response across compliant, 80%-dose and 50%-dose blinded preparations; abandon or redesign the bracketing chemistry if common excipients destroy separation.

## Prototype

Build sealed three-lane cards and a light-tight battery reader with calibration tile, serial/expiry checks and explicit suspect/inconclusive outputs. Test multiple card and reader lots under defined humidity and temperature limits, including accelerated and real-time reference stability. Exit only with a prespecified analytical protocol, reproducible response across operators, and documented failure codes; the US$1.20/card and US$180/reader figures remain unquoted volume targets.

## Pilot

Conduct independent, blinded multi-site comparison with matched Minilab TLC and laboratory HPLC on compliant, ≤80%-dose, absent-API, wrong-API and aged/matrix-challenged tablets. Prespecified provisional gates are ≥90% sensitivity for ≤80%-dose samples, ≥95% specificity for compliant samples and ≤5% invalid controls within labeled storage, reported with confidence intervals by site. Every suspect/inconclusive field result follows local confirmation and medicine-access procedures; no pilot card alone decides dispensing.

## Scale

Proceed only after regulator and quality-system review, independently traceable reference supply, stable manufacturing lots, operator competency and a funded confirmation pathway. Audit real total cost per valid test—including labor, shipping, disposal and laboratory referrals—against the unquoted card/reader targets; reject scale-up if reference integrity, access or false-alert burden deteriorates. Add new APIs and formulations only after separate validation.

## Ubiquity

By 2050, conditional on validated methods and local authorization, integrate traceable screening observations into routine pharmacy inspection and procurement custody records without patient identifiers or a consumer-facing authenticity badge. A mature network would publish aggregate failure and invalid-rate audits, support affordable laboratory adjudication and retain a withdrawal pathway if performance drifts. Adoption is a scenario, not a promised outcome.
