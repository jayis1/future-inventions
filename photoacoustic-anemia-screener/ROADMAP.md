# Roadmap — Photoacoustic Anemia Screener

**Author: jayis1. Research plan only; all gates are proposed, not achieved.** The sequence is conditional: failing a clinical or safety gate stops deployment. See [SPECIFICATION.md](./SPECIFICATION.md) for the acceptance tests.

## Concept

Build 660/850 nm pulsed-LED and PVDF bench rigs with a pressure-limited contact mechanism. Independently vary blood-mimicking absorber concentration, oxygenation, pigment, scattering, depth and flow in tissue phantoms; measure optical exposure at the skin, acoustic bandwidth and coupling-check reliability. **Exit:** show a repeatable blood-linked feature that survives nuisance-variable challenges and an exposure design ready for formal safety assessment; otherwise stop or redesign.

## Prototype

Assemble an offline rechargeable handheld with seating interlock, replaceable pad, motion/pressure rejection and three-state output. Qualify skin-contact materials, cleaning, electrical/optical safety and phantom drift. Only after ethics and safety approval, collect paired same-visit venous CBC data in a blinded feasibility study that includes diverse participants; keep participants separate across model development and evaluation. **Exit:** an independently assessed signal and documented invalid-read causes, not a cherry-picked Hb correlation.

## Pilot

Preregister thresholds and recruitment at multiple clinical sites covering pregnancy, children, skin pigmentation and perfusion variation; obtain consent, clinician oversight and paired CBCs. Report sensitivity, specificity with confidence intervals, missed cases, referral burden, subgroup performance, repeatability and invalid rate on an independent holdout site. **Exit:** meet ≥90% sensitivity, ≥80% specificity in every intended-use stratum and <10% invalid attempts, or restrict the indication and repeat validation. No clinical rollout follows from a single pooled score.

## Scale

After regulatory authorization for each jurisdiction, validate production calibration, cleaning, annual phantom checks, five-year durability and replacement-part supply; target US$100–150/unit at 100,000 units/year and US$20/year service without trading away quality. Audit the full cost of referral/CBC confirmation and independent post-market subgroup outcomes. **Exit:** repeatable, serviceable manufacturing and a funded clinical referral pathway; unit-cost estimates remain unverified until suppliers and field data confirm them.

## Ubiquity

Only if external trials and real-world surveillance continue meeting the gates, integrate triage into consent-based antenatal, child-health and mobile-care programmes over a 10–20 year research-to-deployment horizon. Publish calibration transfer methods, invalid-read rates and adverse-event reporting, keep readings offline by default, and retire unsafe models. **Exit:** independently reproducible performance and access to confirmatory blood testing across intended populations—not merely a large shipment count. The 2050 scenario is conditional, not a promised adoption date.
