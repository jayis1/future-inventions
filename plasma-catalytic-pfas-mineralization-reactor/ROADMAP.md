# Roadmap — Plasma-Catalytic PFAS Mineralization Reactor (PCPMR)

> From concept to ubiquity: a 15-year path to making "forever chemicals" a solved problem.

---

## Phase 1 — Concept & Feasibility (Years 1–3)

Establish the scientific foundation. Bench-scale single-module DBD reactors (50 mL active volume) are built and operated on synthetic PFOA/PFOS solutions at 1–10 µg/L. The central experimental question: does the BaTiO₃/CeO₂ catalyst bed, placed in the plasma zone, lift defluorination above the bare-NTP baseline of 10–20% toward the modeled >90%? Parallel DFT calculations refine the C–F barrier-lowering estimate (0.4–0.8 eV) and the CeO₂ vacancy binding energy for F vs. OH. By year 3, the team publishes peer-reviewed evidence of >90% single-pass defluorination on long-chain PFAS, quantifies the energy budget (target <3 kWh/m³), and identifies the dominant byproducts (F⁻, CO₂, short-chain intermediates) by LC-MS/MS. Output: TRL 3 — component validation in lab.

## Phase 2 — Prototype & Pilot (Years 3–6)

Scale to a 1–10 m³/day community pilot unit. Integrate the ZrO₂/GO reactive membrane and recirculation loop, achieving >99% mineralization across the full PFAS spectrum including short-chain (PFBA) and ultrashort (TFA) species. Test on **real groundwater** from a PFAS-impacted site (e.g., a former AFFF military base) to quantify matrix effects (NOM, hardness, nitrate) and validate pre-treatment and catalyst-doping mitigations. Operate continuously for 6 months to measure catalyst deactivation and demonstrate in-situ plasma regeneration. Integrate the CaF₂ fluidized-bed crystallizer and verify recovered fluorspar purity (>85%) and LiPF₆-grade upgrading. Output: TRL 5–6 — pilot validated in relevant environment.

## Phase 3 — Product & Certification (Years 6–9)

Miniaturize to the point-of-use (POU) form factor: <400 mL, <2 W, <500 g, USB-C/solar-powered. Pursue **NSF/ANSI 53 + 58 certification** (PFAS reduction) for the consumer tap unit. Run consumer trials in 100–500 PFAS-impacted households. In parallel, deploy 5–10 community pilots (10 m³/day) in firefighting-foam-affected communities, refugee camps, and rural well systems, with solar+battery operation. Begin regulatory engagement with EPA, EU Drinking Water Directive, and WHO for municipal-scale acceptance. Output: TRL 7 — POU prototype certified; community system in early commercial deployment.

## Phase 4 — Scale & Municipal Integration (Years 9–12)

Deploy the first **1,000–10,000 m³/day municipal retrofit** at a PFAS-impacted utility, side-by-side with the incumbent activated-carbon plant for direct cost and performance comparison. Full life-cycle assessment (LCA): energy, CO₂, recovered fluorine, avoided hazardous waste. Open-source the community-scale hardware design (reactor geometry, catalyst synthesis recipe, controller firmware) to enable local manufacture in low- and middle-income countries. Secure EPA validation under the PFAS NPDWR and equivalent EU/Asia regulations. Catalyst supply chain scales: BaTiO₃ from abundant Ba/Ti; CeO₂ from rare-earth-refining byproduct. Output: TRL 8 — municipal system commercially demonstrated; open-source hardware available.

## Phase 5 — Ubiquity (Years 12–15)

PCPMR becomes the default PFAS remediation technology globally. Activated-carbon capture-and-haul, with its hazardous-waste tail, is phased out of new municipal specifications. The $80–200 POU unit is sold through consumer channels and subsidized for low-income PFAS-hotspot households. Recovered fluorspar from municipal plants re-enters the LiPF₆ battery and steel-flux supply chain at meaningful scale (50,000–250,000 t/yr globally). Blood-serum PFAS levels in newborns begin declining for the first time since monitoring began. The "plasma + piezo + vacancy shuttle" template extends to other persistent contaminants — pesticides, pharmaceutical residues, microplastic additives — establishing a new paradigm of **destruction-and-recovery** water treatment. Output: TRL 9 — global deployment; "forever chemicals" no longer forever.
