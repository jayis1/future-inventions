# PPD-Free Tread Laminate — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1**

All phases are conditional research gates, not scheduled product launches. See [the blueprint](README.md) and [the scenario model](IMPACT_ANALYSIS.md).

## 1. Concept

Specify PPD exclusion for cap and bonding stock, the matched 6PPD-containing retread comparator, candidate EPDM/SBR/BR morphology and analytical detection limits. Record cure compatibility, ozone, grip, wear, substitute-toxicity and casing contamination as falsification hypotheses. **Gate:** independent reviewers agree on a pre-registered test matrix and go/no-go criteria before mixing.

## 2. Prototype

Make reproducible small batches and bonded coupons, then characterize phase distribution at multiple wear depths, ozone cracking, abrasion, peel/flex fatigue, thermal aging and leachate products. **Gate:** multiple batches pass screening without an earlier crack, unacceptable separation or preliminary grip and toxicity warning; failures retire the recipe rather than relaxing the gate.

## 3. Pilot

Qualify complete retreads on closed track before any independently approved public-road fleet comparison (planning size: 1,000 retreads). Report matched wet-braking, rolling resistance, wear, failure rates, tire-wide 6PPD-quinone and aquatic bioassays per tire-kilometre, including casing sources and blank corrections. **Gate:** applicable safety certification and the blueprint's provisional comparative targets all pass with documented uncertainty; a favorable chemical assay alone is insufficient.

## 4. Scale

Only after the pilot, validate process capability, ingredient chain-of-custody, lot assays, scrap handling and independent lifecycle accounting at the *planning* volume of 100,000 compatible retreads/year. Test the unquoted ≤US$20 incremental cap-cost target against actual supplier quotes and cost per kilometre. **Gate:** no deterioration in certified service life or road safety, and sustained measured chemical and total-hazard performance across production lots.

## 5. Ubiquity

Consider wider licensing and new casing classes only where repeated climate-, fleet- and watershed-level studies independently reproduce safety, cost, exposure and lifecycle outcomes. Keep separate requirements for new tires and retreads. **Gate:** ongoing surveillance shows no worse overall aquatic hazard, particle burden or replacement frequency; withdraw failing variants. A 2050 low-release tire market is a vision, not an adopted product forecast.
