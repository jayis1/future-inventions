# Pressure-Witness Insole — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** Proposed gates, not completed milestones. This insert augments prescribed footwear and foot checks; no phase may claim diagnosis or ulcer prevention without suitable evidence and review.

## Concept

Specify the force-band selection, four-to-six-site placement, high-contrast clear/amber/invalid readout, worker-only reset and safe load path beneath a prescribed cushioning insole. Interview wearers, caregivers and footwear workers; exit only when a clinician can define realistic fitting, referral and invalid-state protocols without changing prescribed offloading.

## Prototype

Mold captive silicone or TPU dome/flag cells with a sealed PET/TPU window and compliant spreader. Bench-test lots over 100,000 load cycles, sweat, bending and temperature, targeting trip force within ±15% of each assigned band; compare with a calibrated in-shoe pressure map in several sizes and shoes. Exit only if no mapped high-risk site gains peak pressure versus prescribed footwear alone and failure states remain legible; otherwise redesign or stop.

## Pilot

After ethics and regulatory review, run a supervised usability and safety study in people with healed plantar ulcers, comparing witness-plus-standard-care with standard care. Pre-register false trips, missed mapped excursions, invalid rates, wear adherence, referral completion, new hotspots and adverse events; require adequate confidence intervals and an independent safety review before a larger trial. An ulcer-prevention claim needs a separately powered clinical outcome study.

## Scale

Qualify lot traceability, biocompatible encapsulation, repeatable fitting and worker training, with quarterly inspection and a target one-year service life. Test the unvalidated $12/pair ex-factory target at 100,000 pairs/year in 2038; budget fitting, follow-up, distribution, disposal and surveillance separately. Do not expand if real-world safety or completed referrals fail the pilot criteria.

## Ubiquity

Conditionally, by 2050 integrate the offline witness into footwear services only where independent trials show net benefit and local follow-up is available. Publish annual failure, adverse-event and replacement audits, retain an invalid-state pathway and withdraw unsafe lots. Wide availability is a scenario contingent on evidence, not a forecast.
