# Roadmap — Refrigerant Reclamation Microcart

**Author: jayis1**

**Status: proposed gates, not completed milestones.** The cart recovers and tracks refrigerant; a qualified downstream facility, not the cart, performs reclamation.

## Concept

Freeze the initial certified nonflammable, conventional-pressure refrigerant envelope and interview licensed technicians and reclaimers about misidentification, cylinder exchange and actual costs. Exit only with an auditable material-flow diagram, a hazard review and a written offtake route for accepted and rejected cylinders; retain the US$3,000 ex-factory goal as an unquoted planning assumption.

## Prototype

Integrate a certified recovery module, analyzer, calibrated scale and physical cylinder-routing gate without claiming certification of the assembly. Bench-test one eligible 1 kg charge type at documented ambient conditions against a maintained kit: quantify residual charge, hose losses, time and kWh/kg; target ≥95% capture where lawful and ≤10 g handling loss, and demonstrate safe response to misidentification, failed scale and power loss. Independent engineering review is the gate to a pilot.

## Pilot

After relevant equipment approval and technician training, run matched service visits at a small group of licensed shops with a contracted reclaimer. Record cylinder IDs, accepted mass, rejects, spill events, actual service cost and uncertainty; test whether the hypothesized +0.1 kg/job accepted mass holds against a conventional kit. Stop if safety, contamination or reclaimer logistics fail.

## Scale

Only after a positive controlled pilot, validate repeatable assembly, calibration and spare-part supply, obtain required certifications in each jurisdiction and contract regional cylinder exchange. Require a documented cost per additional kilogram accepted, a traceable residue-disposal path and a reproducible audit before expanding production.

## Ubiquity

By 2050, offer the workflow as ordinary shared equipment for trained service and take-back operators where affordable offtake exists. Success means independent audits show durable incremental capture without new safety incidents or double-counted climate credits; if simpler existing kits perform equally well, adopt the custody workflow without manufacturing the cart.
