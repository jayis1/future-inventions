# Regenerable Grain Guard Cassette — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** Research concept; milestones are conditional gates, not dates or completed trials. The reference unit is one 1 kg zeolite-13X cassette serving a sealed 50 kg bin of already dried paddy rice (≤13% wet basis). See [README](README.md) for design limits.

## Concept

Model vapor ingress, grain/headspace redistribution, bead sorption isotherms and seal leakage across realistic temperature and humidity cycles. Verify the proposed 80 g usable buffer and 0.2 g/day leak allowance experimentally before finalizing cage and dock drawings. Gate: show that the proposed cassette has a measurable benefit over repairing a plain hermetic bin, not merely over an open sack.

## Prototype

Build guarded cassette, indicator, bin interface and isolated regeneration dock. Test matched 50 kg paddy lots in sealed bins with and without cassettes for 90 days, including an explicitly measured small-leak challenge; log multi-depth moisture, water activity, insects, mold/mycotoxins, mass loss and bead mass. Gate: ≤1% marketable-mass loss as a design target, demonstrably lower loss than matched controls, plus 20 regeneration cycles with measured capacity retention and no barrier failure; measure dock input energy and exhaust safety.

## Pilot

With a willing cooperative or postharvest research partner—not an assumed commercial endorsement—trial 100 paired bins for two storage cycles under a preregistered sampling plan. Include operator training, missed-service cases, rodent damage and a blinded food-contact/dust assessment. Gate: independent analysis confirms benefit, acceptable food-safety and mycotoxin outcomes, and net economic value versus hermetic-only bins including labor, meters, dock and replacements.

## Scale

Only after regulatory and food-contact review, qualify suppliers for traceable beads, durable seals, cleanable cages and repairable shared docks. Audit lot-level QC, service records and counterfeit/spent-module returns; measure actual cost per 50 kg-cycle and material/energy flows in multiple climates. Gate: positive incremental lifecycle value and no unresolved safety failures; otherwise redesign or stop.

## Ubiquity

Extend to cooperative networks only where the pilot's leak/humidity envelope recurs and technicians can run safe off-bin regeneration. Publish matched-control loss and operating-cost data, track discarded cartridges and repair rates, and retest each new crop or container size rather than extrapolating from paddy. Gate: reproducible net benefit at scale; plain hermetic storage remains the default wherever it wins.
