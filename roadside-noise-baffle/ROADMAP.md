# Repairable Roadside Noise Baffle — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1. Proposed gates, not completed milestones.** The full concept and source links are in [README.md](README.md).

## Concept

Survey one candidate 100 m road frontage, its traffic spectrum, receiver heights, openings and opposite-side receptors. Model an equally tall open slat control and a permitted solid screen; proceed only if a 1.5 m line-of-sight break, safe setback, drainage and maintenance access are plausible. Reject sites where geometry or road approvals make the comparison meaningless.

## Prototype

Build 1 m × 1.5 m removable bays with three cavity-insert families and weather-protected necks. Measure impedance and complete-panel transmission/airflow before and after wet, dust and ice cycling; independently test wind, anchor, fire and debris behavior. Gate: ≥30% geometric open area and ≥60% measured air-speed ratio at equal pressure without structural or drainage failure; redesign rather than declare acoustic success from a laboratory resonance peak.

## Pilot

With road-authority approval, test a continuous 100 m frontage against equal-geometry open slats and a compliant solid barrier. Pre-register fixed 1.5 m-high and elevated receivers, matched traffic windows, opposite-side readings and a repeat wet-season test. Gate: ≥2 dB(A) incremental receiver reduction versus slats at the preselected ground-level positions, no material opposite-side worsening, safe operation and a published installed-cost ledger; otherwise stop or retune.

## Scale

Qualify interchangeable inserts, recycled-aluminum suppliers and independent installers with inspection and take-back procedures. Obtain actual fabrication, foundation, replacement and maintenance quotes; compare lifecycle cost with permitted solid barriers and non-barrier mitigations at each site. Gate: multiple independent sites reproduce the pilot criterion and pass local transport-authority safety and maintenance audits.

## Ubiquity

Only after long-term weathering and independent field replication, make the approved module interface and test protocol available to municipal procurement. Periodic receiver-side remeasurement and replaceable parts must remain funded; retire the design at sites where openings, changing traffic or elevated receptors erase its benefit. This phase is an adoption possibility, not a dated deployment claim.
