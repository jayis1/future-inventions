# Scavenge-Exhaust Catalyst Pod — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** Research milestones, not clinical approval or promised dates. See [the concept](README.md) and [impact model](IMPACT_ANALYSIS.md).

## Concept

Audit a representative N₂O-only scavenging stream and existing supply leaks; model heat demand, catalyst compatibility and fan restriction. Exit only with a documented eligible operating envelope, mass-balance protocol and medical-gas engineer's independent bypass requirements; reject if repair or an incumbent unit is preferable.

## Prototype

Build an isolated, nonclinical test rig with humid gas mixtures, removable cordierite-supported catalyst, recuperator and mechanically protected discharge. Measure inlet/outlet dry-gas mass, energy, NO/NO₂, particles, thermal cycling and blocked-bed/power-loss behavior. Stop for unacceptable byproducts, hot-surface risk or failed extraction protection.

## Pilot

With institutional and jurisdictional approval, compare supervised exhaust-only installation against leak repair, approved scavenging and a correctly sized commercial unit at eligible sites. Exit after independently audited 30-day conversion, metered kWh/kg inclusive of idle periods, restriction and worker-exposure checks; no patient-circuit connection or implicit clinical clearance.

## Scale

Qualify suppliers, ageing and catalyst return, production inspection, calibration procedures and local approval pathways. Publish a multi-site life-cycle and cost comparison with uncertainty, and stop expansion if pressure, secondary emissions, service life or net climate results do not meet site-specific gates.

## Ubiquity

Only as a conditional long-term outcome, deploy at facilities where recurring audits demonstrate superior whole-site benefit and maintainable independent safeguards; keep alternatives for sites better served by leak repair, capture improvements or existing destruction units. Continue post-deployment surveillance and withdraw designs that fail ongoing safety or benefit checks.
