# Sealed Pit-Emptying Shuttle — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** These are proposed gates, not a schedule or completed milestones. Read the [research specification](SPECIFICATION.md) and [impact analysis](IMPACT_ANALYSIS.md) before interpreting targets.

## Concept

Work with pit emptiers, a receiving utility and a sanitation engineer to define eligible liquid sludge, safe surface access, gas-screening and 20 L vessel handling. Diagram every contaminated boundary from intake to depot wash bay; review single faults, pressure-vessel rating, local transport permission and a matched Gulper/covered-container comparator. Exit only with an approved hazard and ethics protocol, a real receiving partner and a refusal route for unsafe pits; otherwise redesign or stop.

## Prototype

Build a 24 V, 100 W-rated air-pump and 20 L vacuum-rated vessel assembly with a 16 L fill mark, relief valve, float, barrier, gauge and normally closed dry-breaks. On a water-like simulant at 1.5 m lift, target ≥10 L/min at ≤100 W; document 100 wet coupling cycles with no visible leaks, quantify contact residue and challenge each float/barrier fault independently. Exit only after safe isolation on power loss, overfill and blockage, measured pump/exhaust behavior and reviewed two-worker ergonomics; no contaminated field operation yet.

## Pilot

With worker consent and local approvals, compare 100 eligible pit jobs against a maintained Gulper/covered-container service and accessible vacuum service under matched site conditions. Log refusals, full-cycle labor/travel, spills, swab contamination, gas exposure, energy, costs and mass accepted by the authorized receiver. Exit only if independent review finds lower full-chain contact/spill burden with no dangerous single-fault escape, safe cleaning and an acceptable cost; a bad result terminates or changes the design.

## Scale

If the pilot passes, qualify local replacement hoses, gaskets, valves and pressure-rated vessels; audit trained two-worker servicing, wash-water treatment and receiver capacity over multiple routes and seasons. Get supplier quotations rather than assuming the US$600 ex-factory goal at 10,000/year in 2038 or US$60 extra-vessel goal is achieved. Exit only when real procurement, maintenance, disposal fees, worker protection, licensing and independent safety results support viable legal service at scale.

## Ubiquity

A conditional 2050 goal is that suitable wet pits in narrow-access neighborhoods can receive a dignified, recorded, contained pickup with a verified treatment receipt. Continue auditing spill and exposure events, refused pits, local repair supply and actual receiver mass; retire any unit whose containment fails. Broad coverage is not a substitute for better toilets, larger extraction services or treatment capacity, and it is not a forecast of health outcomes.
