# Smoke-Refuge Witness Cassette — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1** · Research concept; milestones are conditional, not launch dates.

## Concept

Specify the 40 m³ reference room, particle-only use boundary, 200 m³/h smoke-CADR and 35 W targets, cooling and evacuation exclusions, and a failure-mode analysis of bypass, loaded media, blocked witness ports and power loss. Exit only when an independent safety reviewer approves measurable requirements and a test protocol.

## Prototype

Build serviceable gasketed filter frames, the 24 V blower, pressure/flow witnesses and a protected pack. Independently measure smoke CADR, physical flow, noise, electrical draw and runtime with clean, loaded and deliberately misseated filters; demonstrate that faults are detectable or redesign the witness. Exit on repeatable combined-target measurements and electrical/fire pre-compliance review, not a spinning fan.

## Pilot

In supervised homes and lending sites, measure room PM₂.₅ alongside outdoor levels, cooling, usability, filter changes and false-normal witness states, compared with appropriately sized existing cleaners. Recruit users with accessibility needs, preserve evacuation options and stop trials when conditions are unsafe. Exit only after independent review of adverse events, exposure data, maintenance compliance and affordability.

## Scale

Qualify multiple filter suppliers and repair shops, standardize a post-service seal/CADR check, audit battery and charger safety, and publish batch-level quality results. Exit when production variation, consumable costs and end-of-life collection are verified under representative smoke and humidity conditions; revise the US$210 planning target using supplier quotes.

## Ubiquity

If evidence and regulation support it, offer interoperable replacement frames and library loan inventories with accessible, offline instructions. Track verified service rates, filter disposal and measured exposure outcomes across regions; retire or redesign units that cannot be maintained. Wide adoption is a contingent ambition, not evidence of protection.
