# Snakebite Venom Triage Strip — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** Research concept; none of these gates has been met. Clinical care follows established local pathways, not a prototype strip.

## Concept

Define a target product profile with regional clinicians: the two antigen groups, locally stocked antivenom coverage, bite-site swab collection constraints and a no-clearance interpretation. Select candidate VHH binders and independent reference assays through authorized partners. **Exit gate:** documented intended-use population, specimen, regional coverage, reference standard, risk register and ethics plan; abandon panels that cannot answer a useful local clinical question.

## Prototype

Build sealed swab/buffer cards with paired neat/diluted lanes, nitrocellulose capture lines, latex reporters and flow control. Independently evaluate analytical limits by antigen and swab matrix, geographic venom variability, off-target cross-reactivity, interference, high-dose effects, lot reproducibility and packaged heat/humidity stability; do not extrapolate from published plasma or urine experiments.[1][2] **Exit gate:** preregistered analytical criteria and reproducible results meeting them, plus a safe invalid/indeterminate pathway and documented residual risks.

## Pilot

With ethics approval, clinical partners and applicable regulatory permissions, compare *blinded* card results with an accepted clinical pathway and reference assays in prospective patients across relevant sites; strip results must not change treatment in the observational phase. Measure per-group sensitivity, specificity, invalid rates, time-to-care and missed envenoming with confidence intervals and a predefined stopping rule. **Exit gate:** independent review finds adequate safety and usability evidence to justify a controlled strip-assisted trial, not general clinical rollout.

## Scale

Only after a controlled trial demonstrates net benefit without delayed care, seek registration in each intended jurisdiction; qualify manufacturing lots, shelf life, labels, training, procurement, waste handling and supply-chain integration. **Exit gate:** local authorization, verified quality system and funded referral/antivenom capacity, with post-market surveillance and recall processes functioning before routine use.

## Ubiquity

Where repeated region-specific evaluations support use, integrate the card as an adjunct in national protocols and update panels as venom ecology and antivenom availability change. Aggregate surveillance must be consented and privacy-preserving; discontinue regions or lots whose performance deteriorates. **Exit gate:** independently audited outcome and equity monitoring sustains or improves care against usual practice, including remote and underserved populations.
