# Soil Extract Validity Kit — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** All gates are proposed, not achieved. Advance only when independent results justify the next phase; keep the output limited to water-extract nitrate bands or invalid.

## Concept

Test whether a no-color-reagent matrix blank and a known post-filter nitrate-N spike can flag colored or suppressive filtrates without confusing the operator. Define the 10 g field-moist soil / 50 mL water workflow, the intended soil classes, nitrite interference protocol and a visual gate that a color-saturated pad cannot pass; exit when analytical chemists can specify measurable recovery and safe consumables.

## Prototype

Build washable polypropylene hardware and lot-coded paper cassettes; challenge at least 100 diverse soils across sand, loam and clay, plus deliberate wrong ratios, short shaking, high color and aged pads. Compare matched water extracts with laboratory nitrate analysis and laboratory-measured 70–130% spike recovery; publish failure cases, repeatability and operator variability before claiming any pass/fail performance.

## Pilot

Blindly compare at least 300 seasonal field samples, spanning the intended soil classes, with independent matched-extract laboratory results. Require fewer than 10% of reference-high samples to be displayed low without an invalid warning, report the confidence interval and invalid fraction, and pause if referrals cannot be completed. Extension workers may use bands only alongside local agronomic calibration, not as dose prescriptions.

## Scale

Qualify manufacturing at the **unvalidated 2038 targets** of $12 per reusable kit at 10,000 kits/year and $0.55 per test at 1,000,000 tests/year, including lot release and traceability. Validate 12-month shelf life under declared real-world storage conditions and budget separately for labor, distribution, training, repeat tests, waste and laboratory referrals; do not scale if cost per usable decision or false-valid rate fails pilot gates.

## Ubiquity

Only after repeated independent multi-region audits, make the validity flag interoperable with extension records while preserving offline use and farmer privacy. Publish soil-class limitations, annual lot surveillance, referral completion and decision-impact evaluations; withdraw or revise a kit when local calibration or controls fail. A 2050 network is a conditional scenario, not a deployment forecast.
