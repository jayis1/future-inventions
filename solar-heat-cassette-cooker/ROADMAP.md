# Solar Heat Cassette Cooker — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** Research concept; all dates, costs and performance figures are planning targets, not funded commitments or certified results. Advance only through measured safety and cooking performance.

## Concept

2026–2028: co-design a small-meal, evening-use recipe set with cooks; publish a nonproprietary keyed cassette/dock interface and heat/pressure hazard analysis. Calculate the 200 Wp PV → 2.5 kg erythritol → pot energy budget and obtain actual supplier quotes. Gate: independent review finds a plausible expansion/relief design and a test protocol for the 0.25 kWh pot-delivery target.

## Prototype

2028–2031: build instrumented lab cassettes, dock and insulated pot base; meter charging, six-hour standby and calorimetric pot output under specified sun and ambient conditions. Run ≥500 thermal cycles, leakage, drop, latch-jam, overtemperature, child-access and hot-surface tests. Gate: independently repeated ≥0.25 kWh useful pot heat per full charge, ≤0.06 kWh standby loss over six hours, and <50°C accessible surface during ordinary transport, with pressure relief proven safe; failed gates mean redesign, not field sale.

## Pilot

2031–2035: subject qualified devices to relevant ISO 19867 controlled cooking and field-test methods and applicable local electrical, burn and food-contact requirements; the standard does not itself certify the product. In a consent-based 100–500-household trial, compare fuel stacking, PM2.5/CO exposure, meal completion, burns, maintenance, queue times and ownership cost against existing stoves and a conventional electric cooker on the same supply. Gate: no unresolved serious safety defects and verified displacement of a meaningful share of small polluting cooking events without undercooking.

## Scale

2035–2042: if the pilot passes, qualify multiple cartridge suppliers against the open interface and train local inspection/repair shops; expand shared daytime charging racks only where PV area and queue capacity support actual demand. Test the unvalidated $120 cooker/cassette and $220–260 dedicated-PV installed goals against audited landed cost; any carbon-credit or concessional finance must use measured, additional, non-double-counted displacement. Gate: affordable lifetime cost, traceable replacements and independently audited safety and adoption at volume.

## Ubiquity

2042–2050: replicate only in climates and cooking cultures where measured evening-use and backup-fuel patterns justify it. Publish interoperable safety and repair standards, routine service/retirement rules and yearly utilization and exposure audits. Gate: sustained benefits after stove stacking, embodied PV/cartridge emissions and end-of-life impacts are counted; 2050 street-corner exchange is a scenario, not a deployment prediction.
