# Roadmap — Solar Steam Waste Quarantine

**Author: jayis1. Research plan only; no phase has passed.** Progress requires local waste-treatment and pressure-equipment review; a failed gate stops deployment, not merely delays it.

## Concept

Specify the only intended stream—segregated, steam-compatible soft infectious waste—and the excluded sharps, chemicals, pharmaceuticals, anatomical waste, liquids and sealed containers. Model the existing 4 m² collector's thermal budget against the 5 kg maximum load and real cloudy-day profiles; commission an independent hazard review covering pressure, air purge, condensate and quarantine overflow. **Exit:** a reviewed energy/containment model, a worst-case load and indicator test plan, and a credible licensed-vessel procurement route.

## Prototype

Integrate the guarded collector with a certified chamber, independent relief, interlocked door and cold-spot recorder. Measure heat-up, steam penetration and exhaust/condensate containment over light and dense approved loads and deliberate sunlight or sensor failures; challenge worst-case sites with *Geobacillus stearothermophilus* indicators through an independent lab. **Exit:** repeatable logged ≥121 °C cold-spot exposure for ≥45 uninterrupted minutes with passing load-specific biological challenges, fail-closed interruption behavior and pressure-safety sign-off; no clinical waste is released based on a bench demonstration.

## Pilot

Only after site authorization, operate at three to five supervised clinics for a proposed six months, with licensed backup treatment for all failed or overflow loads. Record attempted and qualified batches, indicator results, pressure incidents, staff exposure, water and backup power, total cost, weather downtime and final-disposal receipts; independently audit a sample of records. **Exit:** regulator-accepted efficacy and safety evidence across load and climate conditions with no false releases, plus a funded backup route; suspend use upon any unsafe failure.

## Scale

Qualify regional suppliers, certified pressure components and spare-parts service without treating the US$1,800 ex-factory target as a quotation. Seek each jurisdiction's waste-treatment approval, train and audit operators, and compare full lifecycle cost per qualified kg against licensed electric or centralized alternatives. **Exit:** approved repeatable production, maintained field calibration, documented treatment quality and independently verified economics; otherwise limit deployments to where the alternative is demonstrably worse.

## Ubiquity

By 2050 only as a conditional ambition, integrate validated small-site use into national healthcare-waste plans alongside licensed hauling for excluded streams and bad-weather overflow. Publish failure, emissions and disposal audits, replace worn pressure parts under inspection programs, and withdraw units that cannot meet current rules. **Exit:** sustained independent evidence of safer disposal and affordable service across multiple regions, not unit shipments alone.
