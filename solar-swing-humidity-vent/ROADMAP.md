# Solar-Swing Humidity Vent — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1** · Concept-stage gates, not delivery dates. Advance only on independently reviewable measurements; stop if ventilation or occupant safety is compromised.

## Concept

Publish a heat, airflow and water-balance model for two 0.5 kg dry-silica-gel beds, a 1 m² glazed collector and a 20 m³/h supply path. Include inlet 30 °C/75% RH, four hours at 600 W/m² and eight hours of supply flow as the provisional reference case; compare against an equal-flow plain vent and source repair. Exit only when a documented design review finds a plausible path to 0.20 kg/day and ≥1.5 MJ/day useful regeneration heat without mixing the two air streams; otherwise revise or retire the capacity target.

## Prototype

Build a serviceable window cassette with contained beds, four-way sealed damper, 12 V fan, PV/battery supply, probe cross-check and independent compliant-ventilation bypass. Test whole-day supply/exhaust mass balance, pressure drop, moisture carryover and electrical consumption in a climate chamber, including 500 switch cycles and overcast/saturated failure states. Gate: ≥0.20 kg/day net removal *from incoming air* at the reference case, ≤48 Wh/day auxiliary draw, no detectable exhaust-to-supply leakage within instrument sensitivity, and a safe fault indication; publish uncertainties and failures.

## Pilot

After building-safety and local-code review, install 100 units in eligible repaired rooms with 100 matched equal-flow control rooms through a wet season. Log inlet/supply humidity, room RH and surface temperature, power, sunlight, flow, maintenance and comfort. Compare rooms on pre-registered metrics, including hours above a locally justified RH threshold; independently audit combustion backdraft, rain entry, hot surfaces and noise. Gate: no safety failures, reproducible incoming-air moisture removal, and a statistically interpretable room-level benefit; otherwise narrow the application or stop.

## Scale

Qualify weather-exposed sealing, fire behavior, gel containment, repairability, battery replacement and end-of-life collection with regional partners. Test manufacturing yields and the proposed US$95 ex-factory budget at a stated production volume rather than asserting the cost has been met. Gate: third-party safety review, audited service costs and repeated multi-climate pilots showing where the product beats an equal-flow vent; prohibit rollout where leaks, cold bridges or insufficient ventilation dominate.

## Ubiquity

Only after reliable outcomes and local approval, train installers to repair moisture sources first, measure commissioned airflow, maintain filters monthly and inspect beds annually. Publish an open operating-envelope map, lifetime failure rates, replacement-part availability and independently replicated building outcomes. Gate: sustained performance and safe maintenance across diverse suitable sites, with explicit rejection in unsuitable climates and buildings; broader adoption is conditional, never presumed by the 2050 vision.
