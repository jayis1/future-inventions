# Spectral-Guard Air Disinfector — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1** · Research plan, not a product release schedule. Each gate is conditional on the previous safety and performance evidence; no phase authorizes occupied use by itself.

## 1. Concept

Specify the 50 m³ reference room, filtered 222 nm KrCl optical stack, 12 V / ≤60 W power boundary, exposure-budget algorithm and candidate ozone catalyst. Build a hazard register and a bench optical model. **Exit:** traceable aperture spectra at accessible angles, a credible test plan for ≥5 additional eACH and an independent review of eye/skin and secondary-chemistry hazards.

## 2. Prototype

Assemble a serviceable emitter/filter/baffle with a normally-open access interlock and dark-on-fault controller. Run matched emitter-on/off viable-surrogate decay tests at multiple room positions, including low mixing, and measure ozone, VOC oxidation products and catalyst breakthrough at the lowest allowed ventilation. **Exit:** independently repeatable 50 m³ data with confidence intervals, verified fault injection and applicable weighted-exposure margins; failure means redesign, not occupied deployment.

## 3. Pilot

Only after jurisdiction-specific photobiological, electrical and air-quality review, place a small set of qualified units in assessed rooms while keeping required ventilation and filtration. Repeat site spectral maps, air-quality measurements and surrogate-air-decay tests; record maintenance burden, uptime and user complaints without recording people. **Exit:** third-party confirmation of site-specific safety and effective equivalent airflow over a service interval; infection prevention remains unproven.

## 4. Scale

Qualify interchangeable serialized modules, replacement and e-waste handling, annual recalibration feasibility and service training. Test batch-to-batch filter variation and accelerated aging; validate any antimicrobial-device claims under local law. **Exit:** documented manufacturing and service audit with a defensible path toward the unvalidated US$450 ex-factory target at 10,000 units/year (2038 scenario), without relaxing safety gates.

## 5. Ubiquity

If field evidence and local codes allow, make certified dose, spectral and chemistry records interoperable with building maintenance and equivalent-clean-airflow accounting. Keep a low-resource service model that can fail closed without concealing lost protection. **Exit:** independently audited multiyear outcomes and affordable upkeep across varied building types; broad adoption by 2050 is a conditional vision, not a forecast.
