# Spray Contact Sentinel — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1** · Concept only. Phase gates depend on independent evidence, not calendar dates. No phase grants a medical or safety-clearance claim.

## Concept

Screen carboxylesterase/indoxyl-acetate response to named, locally approved formulations at labeled dilution and to likely interferents; model collector recovery, pouch stability and worker-owned readout. Advance only if a repeatable exposed-versus-blank signal survives sweat/rain controls in contained bench work; exclude nonresponding formulations.

## Prototype

Build about 100 coupon/cassette sets and challenge the defined 10 cm² area with graded deposited volumes including 10 µL, plus blank, heat/UV, leakage and timed-read tests. Advance only after blinded analytical-residue comparisons, independent reader agreement and a prespecified ≥95% alert-or-invalid collector-challenge sensitivity target; disclose invalid and nuisance-alert rates separately. Otherwise redesign or stop.

## Pilot

With occupational-safety and ethics oversight, invite consenting workers in two distinct handling settings for one season, pairing private cassette results with independent residue assessment and usability interviews. Stop for missed challenge patterns, coercion, unsafe reassurance or inadequate waste handling; advance only if the formulation-specific assay and safe response behavior are demonstrated without substituting it for PPE or clinical care.

## Scale

Qualify a regional assembler and independent batch QC for sealed enzyme activity, packaging and shelf life; seek an unquoted US$3.50 ex-factory disposable-set goal at 100,000 sets/year by 2038, excluding distribution, calibration and disposal. Advance only with real-time 12-month stability evidence, audited batch rejects, affordable complete field costs and worker-governed deployment terms; otherwise restrict or stop production.

## Ubiquity

Offer the device only for validated formulation/use combinations as an optional adjunct in PPE and extension programs, with worker-controlled results and periodic revalidation. Continue only while independent monitoring finds no false-reassurance harm and demonstrates beneficial handling changes; evaluate poisoning outcomes separately rather than inferring them from coupon alerts. The 2050 vision remains conditional.
