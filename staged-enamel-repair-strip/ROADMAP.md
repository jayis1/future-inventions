# Staged Enamel Repair Strip — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** Concept-stage gates, not promised launch dates. Each phase can end in redesign or cancellation; fluoride-based preventive care remains the clinical baseline.

## Concept

Specify the intact, non-cavitated lesion population and compare peptide-first staging with P11-4 alone, a mixed film and standard fluoride prevention. Review peptide/polymer exposure, ingestion, saliva washout and the applicable regulatory pathway with dental and safety specialists. Exit only with a testable release specification and a hazard register that rules out unsupervised use.

## Prototype

Make sealed two-stage strips at bench scale; characterize stage-specific release under salivary flow, wet retention and mineral uptake in matched artificial enamel lesions after 28-day pH cycling. Proposed gates: ≥80% sequential release, ≥15% lower integrated mineral loss (ΔZ) than peptide alone and ≥90% backing retention across the 15–25-minute sequence; these are targets, not achieved results. Use cross-sectional microscopy to reject surface-only crusts, and begin real-time 30 °C stability against the 18-month target.

## Pilot

After appropriate permissions and preclinical oral-safety work, run a supervised, randomized, examiner-masked study on confirmed non-cavitated lesions against fluoride-based standard care and a peptide-only arm. Prespecify 6- and 12-month lesion non-progression, adverse events, missed follow-ups and stopping rules. Exit only with credible incremental clinical benefit, acceptable safety, and no signal that cosmetic improvement conceals progression.

## Scale

Validate multi-layer assembly, pouch sealing, batch assay and traceability with a 1-million-strip/year *planning* capacity and US$0.30–0.60 per strip *manufacturing* target, including rejects; these are not supplier quotes or delivered treatment costs. Confirm real-time shelf life, relevant authorization, clinician training, waste handling and unit economics before procurement. Exit only if quality and affordability survive full-cost audits.

## Ubiquity

Expand through dental and community programmes only where longitudinal surveillance shows durable benefit beyond ordinary prevention, equitable screening/follow-up and manageable packaging waste. At two strips per assessed lesion, 1 million strips buy at most 500,000 courses before losses, not 500,000 prevented cavities. Publish independent outcomes and stop or replace the product if a simpler intervention works as well.
