# Steam-Penetration Witness Pack — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1** · Concept only; gates are proposed, not achieved. The pack supplements approved sterilization monitoring and cannot certify sterility.

## Concept

Freeze the intended compatible sterilizer/cycle/load families, mechanical interface, steam-entry geometry, fault states and records policy with infection-control and manufacturer input. Gate: a written hazard analysis, independently reviewed test protocol and manufacturer confirmation that an approved instrument port exists; reject incompatible installations rather than modify a vessel.

## Prototype

Build a 316L cassette with reproducible reference load, heat-rated Pt100/cable and isolated offline logger. Map core lag and cold spots against traceable probes and cycle-appropriate indicators, including deliberate air pockets, wet loads and probe failures. Gate: publish calibration error across 110–140 °C and blinded fault/false-alarm data against preregistered criteria; a bench result is not a clinical release authorization.

## Pilot

With site approval, evaluate only qualified configurations alongside existing monitoring in supervised clinics; do not displace biological, package or Bowie–Dick tests. Gate: independent infection-control review, model-specific safety/regulatory assessment and documented sensitivity, false passes, false alarms, operator time, wet-pack rate and response to unverified traces. Failed configurations remain excluded.

## Scale

If the pilot passes, validate supplier lots, indicator compatibility, cassette cleaning/reuse, probe replacement and traceable calibration intervals. Gate: repeatable manufacturing and cross-site quality audits, complete installed-cost accounting (including labor and reprocessing), training and adverse-event procedures; review applicable standards and approvals before any sale for clinical use.

## Ubiquity

Conditional on durable clinical and regulatory evidence, distribute repairable sets with locally qualified recipes and offline records. Gate: periodic post-deployment audits of calibration, trace completeness, false-pass/false-unverified events and maintenance capacity; withdraw unsafe models or configurations, never treat expansion itself as proof of benefit.
