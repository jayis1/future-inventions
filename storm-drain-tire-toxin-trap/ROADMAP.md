# Storm-Drain Tire-Toxin Trap — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1** · Research milestones, not committed dates or approved deployment.

## Concept

Map eligible curb-inlet geometries and governing flood-capacity requirements with a stormwater utility. Model a 0.25 L/s treatment path, 60 L sand/biochar bed and separate gravity bypass; reject sites that cannot retain the original design-storm drainage capacity. Establish test methods for size-resolved tire particles, dissolved 6PPDQ, head loss, media leaching and spent-media classification before building hardware.

## Prototype

Construct a removable frame, sediment basket and sealed bed with safe mechanical lift points. Challenge bench columns with synthetic and real road runoff, varied 6PPDQ concentrations, dissolved organic matter, salt, sediment and temperature; assay with a validated laboratory method. Exit only after debris-loaded and frozen hydraulic tests pass the authority's flood criteria, no harmful leachate increase is observed, and the proposed ≥70% treated-flow 6PPDQ event-load target is met through a declared cumulative test volume; otherwise iterate or stop.

## Pilot

With drainage and traffic approval, instrument 10–20 suitable inlets in one watershed for at least one wet season and paired untreated controls. Measure influent, treated outlet and bypass flows and concentrations over multiple storms, plus sediment mass, toxicity where authorized, servicing hours and media breakthrough. Progress only if whole-storm load and flood-risk comparisons are independently reproducible without unacceptable maintenance or disposal costs; do not infer fish population recovery from inlet measurements.

## Scale

Submit hydraulics, contaminant results, aging tests and lifecycle cost to the applicable local stormwater technology review pathway (for example Washington Ecology's TAPE process where relevant). Qualify multiple media batches, service crews and approved waste handlers; compare installed lifetime cost and outfall performance with existing inserts and bioretention. Procurement is conditional on site-by-site authorization and independent verification, not a blanket certification claim.

## Ubiquity

If the technology survives multi-season audits and improves over alternatives, prioritize sensitive receiving waters and standardize inlet compatibility, transparent bypass reporting and waste manifests. Continue surveillance as tire formulations change and retire units whose head loss, pollutant release or maintenance burden fails the gate; a widespread network is a conditional 2050 scenario, not a deployment forecast.
