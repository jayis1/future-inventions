# Tailings Crest Dust Shield — Impact Analysis

**Author:** jayis1

**Status:** Illustrative scenario arithmetic, not measured efficacy, a market forecast or a health-risk assessment. No eligible-site count, baseline flux or comparative rock-armor performance has been established.

## Boundary and assumptions

The device is a passive, selectively deployed armor on an independently verified stable cover of an inactive dry tailings embankment. Calculations concern *dust generated at treated source area*, not ambient PM10 concentration, population exposure or illnesses. Wind can shift emissions to untreated ground; measure the whole test boundary before claiming net site benefit.

## Source-mass scenario, conditional on all sites qualifying

Annual gross avoided mass versus bare ground = `A (m²) × F (kg/m²/year) × e × u`; converting kg to tonnes divides by 1,000. This is an *illustrative* site-source difference, not PM10 mass or a measured downwind benefit. Do not add a separate “captured dust” benefit to this avoided emission.

| Case | Calculation for A = 1,000,000 m² | Hypothetical gross avoided dust |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Low | 1,000,000 × 0.02 × 0.20 × 0.70 | 2,800 kg/year = 2.8 tonnes/year |
| Base | 1,000,000 × 0.10 × 0.50 × 0.90 | 45,000 kg/year = 45 tonnes/year |
| High | 1,000,000 × 0.20 × 0.70 × 0.95 | 133,000 kg/year = 133 tonnes/year |

If windy hours or baseline flux halve while other factors hold, base case falls to 22.5 tonnes/year; if only 100 of the hypothetical 1,000 sites qualify at the same treated area, base case falls to 4.5 tonnes/year. These proportional sensitivities must not be read as probability bounds. If the conventional rock cover already outperforms this shield, incremental dust benefit versus that state-of-practice option is negative; its value cannot be computed without matched tests.

## Inputs, cost and what is not counted

The 100 m² pilot implies 400 nominal modules, **US$18,000** for target ex-factory plus installation and **US$500/year** for the maintenance allowance; these exclude landowner approvals, engineering, new cover, runoff channels, sampling, worker protection and disposal. At the hypothetical 1,000,000 m² portfolio scale: 4,000,000 modules, **US$180 million** target upfront and **US$5 million/year** allowance; ten nominal years total **US$230 million**, excluding all omitted costs and any replacement beyond the allowance. Manufacturing at the README target of 50,000 modules/year is far below that hypothetical one-time order; no delivery schedule or price at that scale is established.

No water saving is credited because conventional comparator water consumption is site-dependent; no carbon saving is credited without embodied-material and transport accounting. Recycled feedstock can still leach contaminants or demand energy to process. Report imported mass per area, leachate chemistry, runoff volume, installation exposure, storm damage and life-cycle emissions against a conventional rock/cover control. Do not convert gross dust mass into avoided deaths or illnesses: particle size, chemistry, dispersion, background exposure and exposure-response remain unknown.

## Measurement to replace assumptions

Record baseline and treated source-flux measurements in matched wind-speed/direction bins across a full dry/wet season; use co-located upwind/downwind PM10 and deposition measurements as *additional* exposure indicators, not sole source proof. Pre-register exclusions and wind-bin weighting, publish instrument calibration, sampling uncertainty, contiguous damaged area and days out of service. A licensed engineer must certify stability, drainage, anchor loading and permitted water quality before and during the pilot; stop if adverse effects appear. Recompute this table from measured distributions, qualified site counts and comparator lifecycle bills only after independent replication.
