# Tailings Crest Dust Shield — Roadmap

**Author:** jayis1

**Status:** Research gates, not a deployment schedule. All field work requires site-owner permission, independent geotechnical review and applicable permits.

## Concept

Map windborne source zones and closure-cover constraints on paper; calculate preliminary wind, runoff and anchor load cases and compare with conventional rock armor. Exit when an independent reviewer identifies a physically plausible, inspectable design and no unaddressed dam-safety conflict.

## Prototype

Build 0.5 m × 0.5 m recycled-glass/ceramic modules and test leaching, corrosion, abrasion, UV, freeze–thaw where applicable, wind uplift and rainfall drainage on an instrumented mock cover. Exit only after destructive tests and a documented design revision demonstrate no uncontrolled release, blockage or unacceptable pullout under site-specific design loads; publish raw results.

## Pilot

With formal approvals and an engineered stable cover, install a 100 m² (400-module) removable strip alongside matched bare and standard rock-armored controls for a full windy/rainy season. Exit if source-flux reduction reaches the unverified ≥50% target against each comparator with uncertainty reported, ≥95% modules remain seated, no module escapes, and site runoff/stability limits are met; otherwise redesign or stop.

## Scale

Test several independently approved inactive sites across different wind sectors and tailings chemistry. Compare installed kg/m², lifecycle US$/m², worker hours, runoff and source emissions with conventional closure options; exit only if independent replication shows a justified safety and whole-life advantage, with a certified local repair and disposal protocol.

## Ubiquity

Offer panels as an optional closure retrofit where a site-specific engineer selects them, not as a universal cap or dam stabilizer. Maintain storm inspections, public environmental reporting and end-of-life takeback; continued use depends on verified performance, enforceable permits and the ability to remove or replace modules without impairing the underlying cover.
