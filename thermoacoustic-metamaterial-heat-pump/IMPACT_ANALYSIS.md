## 7. Risk-Adjusted Impact

The projections above assume full deployment. Applying probability-weighted scenarios:

| Scenario | Probability | Units by 2050 | CO₂-eq avoided/yr |
|----------|------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Full success (ubiquity) | 25% | 3–5 billion | 2.5–4.5 Gt |
| Major adoption (primary market) | 40% | 1–2 billion | 1.0–2.5 Gt |
| Niche success (cold + off-grid only) | 25% | 100–500 million | 0.1–0.5 Gt |
| Limited adoption | 10% | <10 million | <0.05 Gt |
| **Expected value** | | | **~0.9–1.7 Gt/yr** |

Even in the "niche success" scenario, the TMHP avoids 100–500 Mt CO₂-eq/yr — comparable to eliminating all of France's annual emissions.

## Summary

The Thermoacoustic Metamaterial Heat Pump targets the single largest building-energy end-use with a technology that eliminates refrigerants entirely, doubles service life, and extends efficient heating to the cold climates where vapor-compression fails. At full deployment it avoids **2.5–4.5 Gt CO₂-eq/yr** — 5–9% of current global greenhouse gas emissions — while democratizing thermal comfort for billions. The risk-adjusted expected impact of **~1 Gt CO₂-eq/yr** places it among the most consequential single-technology climate interventions available.
