# Thermostable Vaccine Microarray Patch — Roadmap

- **Author:** jayis1
- **Status:** Proposed gates, not dates or clinical milestones achieved. Every phase is for one named antigen/formulation; no platform-wide approval is implied. The 10–15-year broad-deployment horizon is aspirational.

## Concept

Screen trehalose/sucrose–PVP/Na-CMC tip formulations for potency retained through drying and after moisture/temperature challenge, alongside delivered-dose mass balance and 500–700 µm needle mechanics. Gate: pick a formulation with a stability-indicating biological assay and reproducible distal-tip loading; abandon any antigen whose activity cannot survive processing.

## Prototype

Build the 400–900-needle, 2–4 cm² patch, 20–35 N dome, 5–10 minute dwell witness and barrier pouch. Test insertion/dissolution across representative skin models, accelerated and real-time stability toward 24 months at 25 °C/60% RH and 30 days at 40 °C, microbial controls and human-factors failure states. Gate: independent repeatability and potency data, not just an intact-looking patch.

## Pilot

Seek early biologic/device regulatory advice, then conduct approved preclinical work and staged active-controlled clinical trials in the intended population. Compare delivered dose, immunogenicity/non-inferiority against the licensed injection, skin safety, completion errors and adverse events; separately demonstrate a product-specific labeled storage envelope. Gate: regulator-accepted benefit–risk and a safe failure/redosing pathway before programme pilots.

## Scale

Qualify a modular line toward 50–100 million patches/year and the applicable national/WHO procurement or prequalification route; validate aseptic production, lot release, packaging integrity, second-source materials and post-market surveillance. Gate: >10 million conforming doses/year before national dependence, plus audited cost per successfully immunized person; the $0.80–1.50 patch/pack target at >100 million/year is not a quotation.

## Ubiquity

If multiple *independently licensed* formulations meet the same gates, regional producers could supply a material share of eligible outreach doses by 2050 while injections and cold-chain products remain available. Monitor uptake, quality drift, wastage, equity and safety continuously; expand only when measured outcomes improve over local standard care, rather than assuming universal replacement.
