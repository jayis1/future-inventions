# Tidal Marsh Sediment Ratchet — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1.** All gates are proposed, not achieved; coastal regulators and affected communities can reject a site at any stage.

## Concept
Measure tidal range, suspended-sediment concentration, grain/floc settling, relative sea-level rise, fish routes and neighboring sediment budgets at candidate sheltered creek margins. Use a flume model to compare hinged and equal-mass fixed-coir drag, including worst-case jammed slats. Advance only when a permanently open bypass can preserve approved drainage and aquatic passage and a plausible sediment supply exists.

## Prototype
Install removable 1 m modules on one permitted short frontage, with untreated and fixed-coir controls and SET–MH baseline stations. Log flood/ebb stages, velocity, debris and slat wear across spring/neap tides and storms. Advance only if the device remains recoverable, neither flood stage nor neighboring scour breaches locally agreed limits, and no entanglement or fish-passage loss occurs.

## Pilot
Replicate in several sediment-bearing estuaries with at least 20 paired cells monitored for at least 24 months. Test the proposed ≥2 mm/year additional *net surface-elevation gain* versus equal-mass fixed coir, while also checking whether the treated cells keep pace with local relative sea-level rise. Stop or remove on contaminated deposits, harmful hydroperiod shifts, downstream erosion or failed passage; a deposition layer alone is not a pass.

## Scale
If independently reviewed ecological and elevation results pass, standardize recoverable parts, installation drawings, assay protocols, maintenance training and permit templates. Obtain complete installed bids, including monitoring and removal, rather than relying on the unquoted US$57/module manufacturing goal. Advance only where multi-site economics and environmental reviews support deployment without displacing harm to neighboring habitat.

## Ubiquity
By 2050, consider inclusion in local shoreline plans only for sediment-bearing, low-energy sites with ongoing SET–MH and adjacent-habitat surveillance. Qualification remains conditional: a 10,000-site × 20 m² illustration treats 20 ha, but only verified sites count as resilient habitat. Preserve funds and authority to maintain, relocate or remove every module; never market the system as a flood barrier or a carbon credit without separate evidence.
