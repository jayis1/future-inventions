# Verified Abandoned-Well Seal — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1** | Research concept; each gate requires licensed oversight and applicable approvals, not merely elapsed time.

## Phase 1 — Concept

Define eligible retired oil/gas well classes, failure pathways, protected-water constraints and comparator practice with regulators and well engineers; specify measurement uncertainty and a stop-work hazard register. Exit only with an independently reviewed test protocol and documented exclusions; redesign if candidate pathways cannot be distinguished safely.

## Phase 2 — Prototype

Bench-test the retrievable packer, steel fittings and optional auxiliary elastomer against representative brines, pressure/temperature cycles and cement interfaces; calibrate instruments and test deliberate leaks in a controlled mock well. Exit only if the cassette is retrievable and failure cases remain detectable; reject incompatible materials rather than hiding ambiguous readings.

## Phase 3 — Pilot

With permits, a licensed crew and an independent verifier, compare 10 screened shallow wells against matched conventional assessment and plugging, documenting baseline/post-work methane mass flux, regulator-prescribed subsurface integrity and 6-/12-month checks. A failed or inconclusive result triggers site controls, engineer-led remediation and retesting, not a success label; stop the pilot for any uncontrolled migration.

## Phase 4 — Scale

Expand only after the pilot to multiple geologies and contractors, with audited fabrication, repeatable calibration, operator training, total installed-cost accounting and long-duration independent monitoring. Exit when repeatability and safety are demonstrated against conventional practice; pause deployment if rework or cross-zone leakage erases the benefit.

## Phase 5 — Ubiquity

Conditional 2050 goal: publicly accountable programs routinely procure independently checked barriers and maintain a funded stewardship/repair pathway for treated wells. This phase is earned only by sustained regulatory acceptance, verified integrity and a responsible owner for every monitoring obligation; otherwise retain proven site-specific approaches.
