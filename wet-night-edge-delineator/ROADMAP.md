# Wet-Night Edge Delineator — Roadmap

**Author: jayis1** · Research plan; dates, costs and outcomes below are proposed gates, not commitments.

## Concept

Specify the 120 × 65 × 35 mm two-sided polycarbonate/aluminum unit, canopy drainage, recessed fouling witness and frangible off-carriageway base. With an authority, choose permissible colors, setbacks, comparator devices and dry/rain angles; run optical and attachment hazard analysis. Exit when drawings, test protocol and a credible breakaway/debris design exist; otherwise stop.

## Prototype

Build bench units and measure luminous-intensity coefficient (cd/lx) at declared angles under dry, spray and continuous rain before and after simulated 12-month UV, abrasion, splash and freeze–thaw exposure. Trial foot retention/breakaway and sub-five-minute insert replacement on a closed course. Exit only if ≥50% new-dry wet return survives aging, the authority's angular criteria are met and no dangerous impact debris is observed; these targets are unverified.

## Pilot

With written road-authority permission, compare a 20-km eligible shoulder corridor against compliant posts, studs and wet-visible markings over at least one wet season. Record 50 m detection at 40 km/h in a closed-course older-driver study, correct edge interpretation, measured speeds, glare, insert loss, cleaning labor and debris. Remove unsafe or misleading units immediately. Exit when independent reviewers find no new hazard and wet visibility plus five-year cost justify a larger study; one season cannot prove a crash benefit.

## Scale

Qualify a production line toward the unverified US$6/unit at 100,000/year target, audited recycled-aluminum sourcing, insert consistency and safe polymer disposal. Publish angular photometry, installation controls, service intervals and actual installed US$/km against local bids. Exit only with authority-approved specifications, repeatable batches, a funded maintenance plan and a multi-season safety evaluation; do not assume road-stud standards automatically cover a shoulder mount.

## Ubiquity

Offer the unit only where independently replicated safety and maintenance evidence favors it over incumbent measures, with local setback/color approval and a replacement/recycling route. A future standard purchase option—not universal installation—is the exit gate: agencies retain alternative tape, studs, posts, protected routes and speed reduction wherever those work better.
